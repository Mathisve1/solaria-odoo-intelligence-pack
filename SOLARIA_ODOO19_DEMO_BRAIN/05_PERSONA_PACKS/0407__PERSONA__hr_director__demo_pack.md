## Priorities
- One employee file instead of scattered records
- Compliant, humane processes
- Self-service that actually reduces tickets

## Likely frustrations
- HR data in five systems and a cabinet
- Manual onboarding checklists
- Privacy anxiety around every new tool

## Business KPIs
Time-to-hire, onboarding completion, HR tickets per employee, leave processing effort, audit readiness

## Demo emphasis
- The employee hub: one record, role-based visibility shown live
- Onboarding activity plan firing on hire
- Self-service moment on a phone

## Relevant Odoo modules
hr, hr_holidays, hr_recruitment (Community), documents for HR files, sign for contracts, appraisal and payroll (Enterprise, payroll always with country validation)

## Suitable Challenger insights
- HR digitisation fails on access design, not on features; who-sees-what is the real project
- Every scattered employee record is a GDPR finding waiting for an auditor

## Likely objections
- Works council and privacy? (governance-first answer)
- Payroll for our country? (never promised without the country module check and expert validation)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Payroll promises
- Surveillance-flavoured monitoring features
- Technical detail

## Opening questions
- Where does an employee's file live today, all of it?
- Which HR process generates the most questions to your team?

## Recommended closing
We will bring our HR privacy checklist to the next session and design the access model before we talk features.
