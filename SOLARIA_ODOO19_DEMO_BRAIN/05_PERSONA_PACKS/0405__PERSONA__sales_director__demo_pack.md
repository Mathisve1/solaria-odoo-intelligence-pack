## Priorities
- Win rate and cycle time
- Pipeline truth for forecasting
- Sellers selling instead of administrating

## Likely frustrations
- CRM treated as a reporting chore
- Quotes assembled from four tools
- Discount chaos at quarter end

## Business KPIs
Win rate, sales cycle length, quote turnaround time, forecast accuracy, activity coverage

## Demo emphasis
- Lead to quotation in minutes, on their product vocabulary
- Portal moment: customer signs and pays online (Community, labelled)
- Pipeline view a director actually uses on Monday morning

## Relevant Odoo modules
crm, sale with quotation templates and pricelists (Community), approvals or Studio approval rules for discount gates (Enterprise, labelled), marketing bridges if in scope

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Quote speed is a win-rate lever, not a convenience: the first credible quote frames the deal
- Forecast accuracy is a data-discipline product; the system must make discipline the easy path

## Likely objections
- Our sellers will not fill in another CRM (usability/adoption)
- Our pricing is too complex (challenge with pricelist demo on their examples)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Back-office depth
- Accounting chapters
- Admin screens

## Opening questions
- How long from first contact to first quote today?
- Where do deals stall, and when do you find out?

## Recommended closing
Give us three of your real quotes; in the follow-up we rebuild them in Odoo with your pricing logic and time it.
