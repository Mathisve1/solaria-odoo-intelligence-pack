## Priorities
- Will my day get easier, honestly
- Fewer re-typing loops
- Not being blamed by a system

## Likely frustrations
- Tools chosen by people who never use them
- Training that evaporates in a week
- Slow screens at the worst moments

## Business KPIs
Minutes per task, clicks per task, error rework, how often they must ask for help

## Demo emphasis
- Their single most frequent task, before and after, timed
- The mobile or barcode moment if operational
- Questions welcomed mid-demo; this audience decides adoption

## Relevant Odoo modules
Whatever their task touches; depth on one flow beats breadth on ten

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Systems fail at the tenth click, not at the demo; we count clicks on purpose
- The best training is a task that explains itself

## Likely objections
- The old way works fine (respect it, then show the same task faster)
- What if I make a mistake? (undo, drafts, and human-friendly states shown)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Feature breadth
- Management dashboards
- Jargon of any kind

## Opening questions
- Which task would you delete from your week if you could?
- What does the current system make you re-type?

## Recommended closing
You saw your task done in half the clicks; in the pilot, two of your colleagues test it on real work and tell us the truth.
