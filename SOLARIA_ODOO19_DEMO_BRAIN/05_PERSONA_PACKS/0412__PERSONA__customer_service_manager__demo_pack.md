## Priorities
- SLA performance
- Backlog under control
- Agents empowered to actually solve, not just log

## Likely frustrations
- Tickets that need three other systems to resolve
- No visibility on repeat issues
- Tools agents work around

## Business KPIs
First response time, resolution time, SLA hit rate, CSAT, reopen rate

## Demo emphasis
- Email to ticket to SLA countdown (Enterprise, labelled)
- The ERP bridge wow: refund or return created from the ticket itself
- The team lead's Monday view

## Relevant Odoo modules
helpdesk (Enterprise) with stock/account bridges, knowledge for answers, livechat; edition gate stated for Community clients

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Resolution time hides system-hopping time; the expensive part of a ticket is the swivel chair
- Deflection without a governed knowledge base just moves the queue

## Likely objections
- We already have a ticketing tool (bridge story: tickets that act on the ERP)
- Community client? (honest edition gate and options)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Menu tours
- Manufacturing depth
- Unlabelled Enterprise assumptions

## Opening questions
- How many systems does an agent touch to resolve your most common ticket?
- Which SLA do you miss most often, and why?

## Recommended closing
List your top five ticket types; next session we resolve each one end to end, timed.
