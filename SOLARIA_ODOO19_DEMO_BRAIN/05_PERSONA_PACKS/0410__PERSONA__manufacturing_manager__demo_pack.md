## Priorities
- On-time delivery
- Scrap down, utilisation up
- Operators executing without paper travelers

## Likely frustrations
- Whiteboard scheduling
- BoMs living in spreadsheets
- Finding out about material shortages at the machine

## Business KPIs
OTD, scrap rate, OEE direction, changeover time, schedule adherence

## Demo emphasis
- MO flow with component availability visible
- Shop Floor tablet moment: start, instructions, quality check inline (Enterprise, labelled)
- The operator change-proposal beat (lean culture hook)

## Relevant Odoo modules
mrp (Community core), mrp_workorder Shop Floor, quality, mrp_mps (Enterprise, labelled); honest APS boundary always

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Most late orders are late at release time, not at the machine; visibility at planning beats speed at execution
- Paper travelers hide the knowledge your best operators carry

## Likely objections
- Finite scheduling? (honest boundary: sequencing yes, APS no, integration if needed)
- Our BoM data is a mess (true project risk; name it as the workstream it is)

## Unsuitable demo content

- Retail storylines
- Financial deep dives

## Opening questions
- When an order will be late, when do you currently find out?
- Where do your operators get their instructions today?

## Recommended closing
