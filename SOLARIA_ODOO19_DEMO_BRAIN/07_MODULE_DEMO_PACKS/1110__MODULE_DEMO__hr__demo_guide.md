## Demo purpose
One employee file as the hub: org, roles, self-service, and the honest payroll boundary.

## Best personas
HR director, HR officers, works-council-minded stakeholders

## Prerequisites
- Departments/jobs reflecting the client
- employee records with photos
- privacy story prepared

## Minimum demo data
- 25+ employees across departments
- one staged onboarding case
- role-based access users to switch between

## Recommended flow
- The employee card as hub
- who-sees-what shown live by switching users
- onboarding activity plan firing
- self-service leave beat (sibling app)

## Wow moments
- The access-design moment (HR sees X, manager sees Y, employee sees Z, live)
- onboarding checklist firing on hire

## Common mistakes
- Payroll promises without country validation
- surveillance-flavoured framing
- data-privacy questions answered casually

## Standard vs custom notes
- Org, plans, self-service scope: configuration
- payroll: Enterprise plus country module plus expert validation, always
- works-council specifics: design

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Core HR Community; payroll, appraisals, salary configurator, HR documents are Enterprise

## Likely objections
- GDPR/works council (governance-first)
- our country payroll (never promised blind: catalog check plus experts)

## Validation checklist
- Privacy classification of fields
- payroll boundary decision per country
- self-service scope with a test login

## Backup flow
Second onboarding case staged; access-switch users pre-logged-in
