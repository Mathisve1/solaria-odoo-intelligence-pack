# Module Demo Pack: Sales (`sale`)

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Edition | Community |
| Category | MODULE_DEMO (demo guidance only; product truth lives in the Intelligence Pack module docs, routed via foundation 0012) |
| Rule | Every claim keeps its edition tag and claim label (foundation 0018); nothing here is demo-ready until rehearsed (foundation 0017) |

## Demo purpose
Quote-to-order as the deal-winning arc: fast quotation, customer signs and pays online, order confirms itself.

## Best personas
Sales director, CFO (policy moment), CEO (cycle time)

## Prerequisites
- Products with the client's price logic in pricelists
- quotation template with their sections
- portal payment provider sandbox

## Minimum demo data
- Hero quote pre-built plus one built live
- pricelists reproducing 2 real client pricing cases
- customer with portal access

## Recommended flow
- Opportunity to quotation with template
- pricelist applies customer price
- send
- customer view: sign and pay in portal
- order confirmed, delivery spawned

## Wow moments
- The portal signature moment (rehearse!)
- pricelist computing their real price logic
- invoicing policy toggled: ordered vs delivered explained in one sentence

## Common mistakes
- Building a 20-line quote live
- skipping the portal (the best beat)
- price logic hand-waved instead of configured

## Standard vs custom notes
- Templates, pricelists, portal sign/pay: configuration on Community
- discount blocking gates: Approvals or Studio rules (Enterprise) or custom, said honestly

## Community vs Enterprise notes
- Core arc Community
- subscriptions, rental, margins-reporting uplift are Enterprise, labelled

## Likely objections
- Our pricing is too complex (rebuild one real case live or in follow-up)
- legal wants special quote docs (template + validation)

## Validation checklist
- Client's 3 hardest pricing cases in pricelists
- portal flow with their payment provider
- quote template legal review

## Backup flow
Second staged quote if portal payment fails; pre-signed record to show the after-state
