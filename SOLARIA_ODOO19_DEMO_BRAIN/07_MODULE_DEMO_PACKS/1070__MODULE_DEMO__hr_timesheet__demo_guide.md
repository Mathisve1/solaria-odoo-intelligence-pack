## Demo purpose
Two minutes a day to a billed hour: capture friction removed, the money chain visible.

## Best personas
Project managers, consultants (end users), CFO

## Prerequisites
- Projects/tasks to book on
- sale_timesheet for billing beat
- grid view if Enterprise (labelled)

## Minimum demo data
- 3 weeks of entries across 4 people
- one unbilled bucket
- billing rates configured

## Recommended flow
- Log from the task in seconds
- week view
- approve (E grid, labelled)
- invoice from validated time

## Wow moments
- Entry-to-invoice line traceability
- the unbilled-hours flush
- mobile entry moment

## Common mistakes
- Framing as surveillance
- skipping the billing payoff
- demoing empty weeks

## Standard vs custom notes
- Capture and billing mapping: standard/configuration
- validation workflow and reminders: Enterprise
- exotic rate engines: custom, flagged

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Capture Community; grid UX, validation, reminders Enterprise

## Likely objections
- Consultants will revolt (show the 2-minute reality)
- our rates are complex (rebuild one rate card)

## Validation checklist
- Rate logic vs sale items on real contracts
- approval chain decided
- adoption plan owned

## Backup flow
Pre-filled week ready to approve
