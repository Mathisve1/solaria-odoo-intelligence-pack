# Demo Storyline: Document-to-Approval

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|

## Client problem
Documents wander through inboxes; approvals happen in email archaeology.

## Audience
CFO, COO, admins, legal-adjacent; 30 to 45 minutes

## Prerequisites
- documents (E) workspaces with actions
- approvals (E) categories
- sign (E) template

## Modules (edition)
- documents (E)
- approvals (E)
- sign (E): an Enterprise productivity storyline, gate stated upfront

## Start state
Three PDFs dropped into the inbox workspace

## End state
A bill in the AP queue, a contract approved and signed, everything filed with a trail

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. Drag three files
2. file one becomes a vendor bill via workflow action (OCR labelled)
3. file two routes into an approval request, approver acts from activity
4. approved contract goes to signature, signed on the phone
5. AI auto-filing beat with review shown (labelled)
6. the audit trail scroll

## Wow moments
- Three files, three destinies
- approve-then-sign chain
- the trail

## Challenger insight
Document chaos is a process-entry problem: the inbox should be a router, not a residence.
