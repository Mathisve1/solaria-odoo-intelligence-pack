# Demo Storyline: AI-Assisted Process

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | STORYLINE (instantiates the 12-beat framework, foundation 0009) |
| Rules | Timing limits per format apply (0014); every beat needs data (0016) and rehearsal (0017); editions and claim labels always (0011, 0018) |

## Client problem
The CEO wants AI; the teams fear either the hype or the risk; nobody trusts vendor promises.

## Audience
CEO, CFO, CIO; 30 to 45 minutes; governance-forward

## Prerequisites
- Enterprise AI gates validated (edition, pgvector, IAP)
- EVERY ai moment rehearsed same-day
- fallback recording
- governance one-pager ready

## Modules (edition)
- OCR extract family (E)
- ai documents sorting (E)
- drafting assists (E)
- everything labelled; concepts introduced as Deloitte concepts

## Start state
A stack of real-ish vendor bills and a messy document inbox

## End state
Bills processed through the validation queue; the honest map of what is product versus concept

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. PDF bill to filled bill, the validation queue as the hero
2. messy inbox auto-sorted, review step shown
3. one drafting assist in context
4. the honesty beat: what does NOT exist natively (credibility spike)
5. the Deloitte concept slide, labelled as concept
6. governance close: register, human-in-the-loop, pilot path

## Wow moments
- Bill filled in seconds WITH the human check
- the honesty beat itself (rooms remember it)

## Challenger insight
AI in the ERP wins as boring plumbing with checkpoints, not as magic: the validation queue is the feature, not the apology.

## Likely questions
- Accuracy? (pilot on your data, never a number today)
- data boundaries? (contractual validation)
- what would Deloitte build?

## Risks
- First-run AI live (never)
- concept-product blur (never)

## Validation points
- Gates checked
- same-day rehearsal log
- risk/legal review of the AI narrative

## Short version (15 min)
One bill through the queue, one honesty beat

## Executive version (30 min)
Hype-versus-value framing, the queue run, governed-pilot close
