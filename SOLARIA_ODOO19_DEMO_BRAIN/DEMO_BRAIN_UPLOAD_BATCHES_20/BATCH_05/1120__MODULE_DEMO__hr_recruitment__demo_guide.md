## Demo purpose
Attract-to-hire with speed and dignity: pipeline discipline, parsing assist (labelled), a talent pool that is an asset.

## Best personas
HR director, recruiters, compliance owner

## Prerequisites
- Jobs published (website bridge)
- stages per job
- sample CVs tested with parsing same-day (E)

## Minimum demo data
- 2 open positions
- 15 applicants across stages
- refusal templates
- one offer staged for e-sign (E)

## Recommended flow
- Application arrives from the site
- CV parsed into the form (E, labelled, human reviews)
- interview activity loop
- respectful refusal with template
- hire creates the employee

## Wow moments
- CV-to-form parsing with the human validation shown (E)
- the talent-pool save
- offer signed on a phone (E)

## Common mistakes
- Accuracy promises
- autonomous-screening vibes (EU AI Act high-risk: human decides, say it)
- ignoring candidate-data retention

## Standard vs custom notes
- Stages, templates, pools: configuration
- parsing/AI assist: Enterprise with governance
- assessment platforms: integration

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Pipeline Community; OCR parsing, AI assist, referrals, salary configurator Enterprise

## Likely objections
- AI fairness (human-decision governance, transparency)
- job boards (per-board integration checks)

## Validation checklist
- Parsing pilot on their real CV sample
- retention/anonymisation policy configured
- interviewer access tested

## Backup flow
Pre-parsed applicant ready; manual-entry path as honest contrast
