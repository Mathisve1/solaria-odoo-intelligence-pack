# Module Demo Pack: Sign (e-signature) (`sign`)

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Edition | Enterprise |
| Category | MODULE_DEMO (demo guidance only; product truth lives in the Intelligence Pack module docs, routed via foundation 0012) |
| Rule | Every claim keeps its edition tag and claim label (foundation 0018); nothing here is demo-ready until rehearsed (foundation 0017) |

## Demo purpose
Signatures inside the flow: minutes instead of days, with the legal nuance handled like professionals.

## Best personas
Sales director, HR director, legal-adjacent, CFO (cycle time)

## Prerequisites
- Enterprise database
- template with roles on a real-ish document
- phone for the signer moment

## Minimum demo data
- One offer/contract template with fields per role
- a staged request mid-flight
- completed doc with audit trail

## Recommended flow
- Template with drag-and-drop fields
- send to two roles
- sign on the phone live
- status flips, audit log shown, document filed

## Wow moments
- The phone signature landing in the room
- the audit trail scroll
- filed-automatically beat (documents bridge)

## Common mistakes
- 'Legally binding' blanket claims (SES/AES/QES nuance, legal validation per document class)
- demoing without a phone

## Standard vs custom notes
- Templates, roles, reminders: configuration
- QES/identity flows: validation/integration per jurisdiction

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Enterprise-only as a product; Community has only simple quote acceptance, distinguish precisely

## Likely objections
- Is it legal? (nuanced answer with validation)
- we use DocuSign (zero-integration inside-the-flow argument, honest)

## Validation checklist
- Document classes mapped to signature levels with legal
- signer UX tested externally
- retention path

## Backup flow
Pre-signed twin document; request staged one click from completion
