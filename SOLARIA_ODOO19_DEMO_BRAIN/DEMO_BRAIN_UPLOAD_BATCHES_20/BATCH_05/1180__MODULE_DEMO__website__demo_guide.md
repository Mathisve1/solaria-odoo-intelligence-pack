## Demo purpose
The front office on the same platform: edit live, capture demand into real records.

## Best personas
Marketing lead, CEO (brand-to-pipeline), digital manager

## Prerequisites
- Site themed client-flavoured (logo, colors, 3 pages)
- form wired to CRM

## Minimum demo data
- Homepage, product/services page, contact page
- one form feeding leads
- visitor data if shown

## Recommended flow
- Edit a page live (builder)
- publish
- submit the form as a visitor
- the lead exists in CRM with source

## Wow moments
- Live edit-and-publish in seconds
- form-to-lead with attribution
- multi-site switch if relevant

## Common mistakes
- Pixel-perfect brand promises (prototype honesty)
- demoing a default theme with lorem ipsum

## Standard vs custom notes
- Builder, menus, forms: configuration
- design systems beyond themes: development, honest
- AI site generation: Enterprise, labelled, rehearsed

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Builder and multi-site Community; website Studio, AI generation, appointments are Enterprise

## Likely objections
- SEO parity with our CMS (boundary honesty, coexistence option)
- performance (hosting validation)

## Validation checklist
- Homepage prototype approved by brand owners
- redirect/SEO plan if migrating
- multi-site data sharing choices

## Backup flow
Second prepared page; published twin of the live edit
