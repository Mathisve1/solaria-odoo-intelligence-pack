## Demo purpose
The inbox that runs itself: files become business records through workflow actions.

## Best personas
CFO (AP inbox), HR director (files), operations, legal-adjacent

## Prerequisites
- Enterprise database
- workspaces with actions configured
- OCR bridge if the bill beat is shown

## Minimum demo data
- 3 staged PDFs (a bill, a contract, a misc file)
- workspace tree matching the client's world
- one document request case

## Recommended flow
- Drag three PDFs
- one becomes a vendor bill (OCR, labelled), one a signed-contract flow, one gets filed by AI sorting (labelled)
- show the access design

## Wow moments
- Three files, three destinies, one minute
- the AI auto-filing with the review step shown (E, labelled)

## Common mistakes
- SharePoint-replacement overclaim (boundary honesty: process documents yes, authoring collaboration no)
- retention promises

## Standard vs custom notes
- Workspaces, tags, actions: configuration
- retention/certified archiving: design/integration, flagged

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Enterprise-only; Community has attachments-on-records only, say it plainly

## Likely objections
- We have SharePoint (coexistence boundary)
- legal retention (design work with legal)

## Validation checklist
- Workspace/access matrix with real roles
- priority workflow actions demoed
- leakage test before AI-on-documents

## Backup flow
Second set of staged files; pre-executed action results in a tab
