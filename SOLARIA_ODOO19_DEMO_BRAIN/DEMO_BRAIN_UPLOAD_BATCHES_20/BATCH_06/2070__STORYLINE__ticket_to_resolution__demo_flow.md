# Demo Storyline: Ticket-to-Resolution

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|

| Rules | Timing limits per format apply (0014); every beat needs data (0016) and rehearsal (0017); editions and claim labels always (0011, 0018) |

## Client problem
Support drowning in email; every resolution needs three other systems.

## Audience
Customer service manager, COO; 45 minutes

## Prerequisites
- helpdesk (E) configured with SLA
- bridges to stock/account staged
- canned responses

## Modules (edition)
- helpdesk (E, gate stated)
- stock and account bridges (E)
- knowledge (E) if answer-insertion shown

## Start state
Customer email about a damaged delivery

## End state
Return created, credit note issued, ticket solved, rating requested: all from the ticket

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. Email becomes ticket with SLA clock
2. agent answers with canned response plus knowledge insert
3. the bridge beat: return order and credit note from the ticket itself
4. solve, rating email fires
5. manager view: SLA and workload

## Wow moments
- The ERP bridge (three systems' work in one screen)
- SLA breach surfacing
- rating loop

## Challenger insight
The expensive minutes in support are the swivel-chair minutes: resolution time hides system-hopping time.
