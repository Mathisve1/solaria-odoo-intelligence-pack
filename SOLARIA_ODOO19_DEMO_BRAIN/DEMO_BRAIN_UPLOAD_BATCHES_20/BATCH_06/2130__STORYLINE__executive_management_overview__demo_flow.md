## Client problem
Leadership flies blind between month-ends; every meeting starts with whose-number-is-right.

## Audience
CEO, CFO, board-level; 30 minutes, dashboard-first

## Prerequisites
- History across the demoed flows
- spreadsheet dashboard (E, labelled) built and populated
- drill paths rehearsed

## Modules (edition)
- Live pivots (C)
- spreadsheet dashboards and statutory reporting labelled (E)
- the operational modules feeding them

## Start state
The Monday-morning management view open

## End state
One number challenged, drilled to its source document, trust demonstrated

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. The management sheet: sales, cash, operations at a glance
2. pick the number an executive would doubt
3. drill: figure to pivot to document
4. change something operational live, watch the sheet move
5. the honest statutory boundary (account_reports E, country validation)
6. close on decision speed

## Wow moments
- The live drill from KPI to source
- the sheet moving after an operational change

## Challenger insight
Decision latency is the invisible cost center: numbers argued about are decisions postponed.

## Likely questions
- Our BI stack? (boundary honesty)
- statutory outputs? (validation)
- who builds these views?

## Risks
Dashboards on thin data (populate or do not show)

## Validation points
- The ten reports that run their business, with samples
- definitions workshop
- BI boundary

## Short version (15 min)
One dashboard, one drill, one live change
