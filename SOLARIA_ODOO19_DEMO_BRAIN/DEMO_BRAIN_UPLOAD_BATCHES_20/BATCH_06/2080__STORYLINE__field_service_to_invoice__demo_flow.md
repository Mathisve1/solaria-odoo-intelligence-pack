# Demo Storyline: Field-Service-to-Invoice

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | STORYLINE (instantiates the 12-beat framework, foundation 0009) |
| Rules | Timing limits per format apply (0014); every beat needs data (0016) and rehearsal (0017); editions and claim labels always (0011, 0018) |

## Client problem
Paper worksheets, unbilled interventions, dispatch by memory.

## Audience
Service director, dispatchers, CFO; 45 to 60 minutes, phone on screen

## Prerequisites
- industry_fsm (E) rehearsed ON THE DEVICE
- worksheet template
- van stock
- signature-ready customer

## Modules (edition)
- industry_fsm (E, on the Enterprise project/timesheet layer)
- helpdesk intake (E)
- stock for parts (C core)

## Start state
Escalated ticket needing an on-site intervention

## End state
Signed worksheet, parts consumed, invoice issued on the doorstep

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. Ticket escalates to field task
2. dispatcher assigns on the map
3. phone: start timer, follow worksheet, add parts from the van
4. customer signs on the screen
5. stop timer, invoice from the task
6. office view: intervention fully accounted

## Wow moments
- The whole phone run
- signature on the doorstep
- invoice before the van starts

## Challenger insight
The most expensive intervention is the one you never invoice: doorstep capture beats office reconstruction every time.

## Likely questions
- Offline? (honest envelope answer)
- route optimization? (integration boundary)
- device cost?

## Risks
Unrehearsed device flow (the single most fragile demo in the portfolio: rehearse ruthlessly)

## Validation points
- Device pilot in real coverage
- worksheets per intervention type
- parts logistics

## Short version (15 min)
Phone run only: start to signed to invoiced

## Executive version (30 min)
Unbilled-work framing, the phone run, revenue-capture close
