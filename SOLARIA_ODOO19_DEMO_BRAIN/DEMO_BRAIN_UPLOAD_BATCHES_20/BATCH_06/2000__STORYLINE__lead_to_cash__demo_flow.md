# Demo Storyline: Lead-to-Cash

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | STORYLINE (instantiates the 12-beat framework, foundation 0009) |
| Rules | Timing limits per format apply (0014); every beat needs data (0016) and rehearsal (0017); editions and claim labels always (0011, 0018) |

## Client problem
Demand leaks between marketing, sales and finance: leads unworked, quotes slow, invoices late.

## Audience
CEO, sales director, CFO; 45 to 60 minutes

## Prerequisites
- crm + sale + account flow rehearsed
- portal payment sandbox
- pipeline history for the manager view

## Modules (edition)
- crm (C)
- sale (C)
- account (C)
- reporting uplift labelled (E) where shown

## Start state
A fresh inbound email from the spine customer; pipeline populated with history

## End state
Paid invoice traced back to the original email; manager pivot showing the funnel

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. Email arrives, lead created with zero typing
2. qualify to opportunity, activity discipline shown
3. quotation from the opportunity with template and pricelist
4. customer signs and pays in the portal
5. order confirms, invoice follows policy
6. payment lands, AR view updates
7. drill the funnel pivot from lead source to cash

## Wow moments
- Email-to-lead
- portal sign-and-pay
- source-to-cash drill-down

## Challenger insight
Demand leaks at handoffs, not at the top of the funnel: every re-typing step between tools is where deals cool off.

## Likely questions
- Lead routing rules?
- discount control?
- what changes for our sellers day one?

## Risks
- Portal payment sandbox fragility (backup: pre-paid twin)
- pipeline history looking fake if rushed

## Validation points
- Client's pricing in pricelists
- portal flow with their PSP
- lead-source taxonomy

## Short version (15 min)
Email-to-lead, quote from template, portal signature; skip manager analytics

## Executive version (30 min)
Compress beats 6 to 8 to one continuous run; expand the funnel-truth and decision-speed framing
