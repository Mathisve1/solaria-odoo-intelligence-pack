# Challenger Scenario: We already use Microsoft / Dynamics

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Microsoft-first IT strategy; Dynamics or the Power platform framed as the default answer.

## Underlying concern
Ecosystem coherence, existing licences, IT familiarity.

## Challenger insight
Platform-default decisions hide integration and customisation effort inside familiar branding: the suite is coherent at the infrastructure layer, less so at the business-process layer where implementations actually hurt.

## Reframe
Judge on the business-process fit and total change effort, not on logo familiarity; coexistence is normal (Office and Odoo live together fine).

## Recommended response pattern
- Concede ecosystem strengths honestly
- move the conversation to concrete processes and demo them
- avoid feature-table wars; offer a side-by-side on their three real scenarios

## Demo action
Run one of their real processes end to end; let the contrast be experienced, not asserted

## Advancing question
If we run your three hardest processes in both candidates on your data, would that comparison settle it?

## Never say
Fabricated Dynamics weaknesses; licensing guesses; 'Microsoft is legacy' style bravado

## Validation needed
Any claim about their Microsoft licensing position is theirs to state, not ours to assume
