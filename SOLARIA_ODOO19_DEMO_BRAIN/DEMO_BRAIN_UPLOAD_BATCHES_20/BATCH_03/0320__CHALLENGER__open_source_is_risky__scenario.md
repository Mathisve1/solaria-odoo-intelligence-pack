# Challenger Scenario: Open source is risky

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Security/procurement stakeholders equate open source with unsupported hobby software.

## Underlying concern
Support accountability, security posture, longevity.

## Challenger insight
Inspectability is a security property, not a risk: closed-source ERP risk hides in opacity. The commercial reality is a vendor-backed product (Odoo SA) with releases, security process and a partner network; the licence model is not the support model.

## Reframe
Evaluate the support chain and security process like any vendor: who patches, who answers, who is liable, what the SLA says.

## Recommended response pattern
- Separate licence from support: Enterprise subscription and Deloitte delivery ARE the accountability chain
- offer the security annex (hosting, patching, access model)
- reference the inspectability advantage calmly

## Demo action
Show the security surfaces (roles, record rules, audit trail) live in the technical annex

## Advancing question
Which specific security or support scenario worries you most: shall we walk exactly that one in the annex session?

## Never say
'Open source is always more secure' absolutism; dismissing the concern as outdated

## Validation needed
Hosting and patching specifics per deployment model (Requires technical/commercial validation)
