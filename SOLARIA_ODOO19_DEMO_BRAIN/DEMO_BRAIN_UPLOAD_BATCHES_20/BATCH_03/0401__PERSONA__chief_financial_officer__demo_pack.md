## Priorities
- Faster, controlled close
- Margin visibility per product, project or channel
- Total cost of ownership honesty
- Audit-proof control environment

## Likely frustrations
- Excel as the real finance system
- Manual reconciliation effort
- Surprises found weeks after they happened

## Business KPIs
Days to close, DSO/DPO, margin by dimension, forecast accuracy, audit findings, finance FTE per revenue

## Demo emphasis
- Invoice-to-payment and the bank reconciliation moment (Enterprise, labelled)
- Drill-down from P&L figure to journal to document
- Controls: lock dates, approval trails, audit log
