## Priorities
- Growth without chaos: one platform instead of tool sprawl
- Decision speed: trustworthy numbers without waiting for month-end
- Focus: leadership time on customers, not on system firefighting

## Likely frustrations
- Every department reports different numbers
- Transformation projects that overran before
- IT topics reaching their desk that should never reach their desk

## Business KPIs
Revenue growth, EBITDA, cash conversion cycle, time-to-decision, employee productivity

## Demo emphasis
- One continuous end-to-end flow (beats 6 to 9 of the storyline)
- The management view: live drill-down from KPI to document
- Implementation realism in 30 honest seconds (builds trust)

## Relevant Odoo modules
The spine flow modules for their industry plus reporting: live pivots (Community), spreadsheet dashboards and statutory reporting (Enterprise, labelled)

## Suitable Challenger insights
- The cost of tool sprawl is not licences, it is the re-typing and the disputes between numbers
- Decision latency compounds: every day of stale numbers delays every decision built on them

## Likely objections
- Is this serious enough for our size and ambition? (scalability category)
- What did comparable companies achieve? (reference proof)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Settings and configuration screens
- Field-level walkthroughs
- Anything longer than 30 seconds without a business point

## Opening questions
- What decision took you the longest last quarter because the data was late?
- Where does your leadership team spend time that a system should be spending?

## Recommended closing
Replay the three takeaways against their three challenges, then: If we prove this on your own data in a focused POC, who on your team should own it?
