# Challenger Scenario: AI is not relevant for us

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Pragmatic stakeholders dismiss AI as hype that does not apply to their business.

## Underlying concern
Distraction from real problems; past disappointment with buzzwords.

## Challenger insight
The applicable AI here is boring and immediate: documents that type themselves into the system (bills, CVs, receipts) with a human check. That is productivity plumbing, not moonshots, and it is measurable in weeks.

## Reframe
From 'AI strategy' to 'which typing work disappears this quarter'.

## Recommended response pattern
- Agree the hype deserves scepticism
- show the narrow, native, validated use cases only (Enterprise, labelled, piloted)
- anchor on their document volumes and the touchless-rate pilot

## Demo action
One vendor bill through the OCR validation queue, rehearsed same-day

## Advancing question
How many vendor bills and CVs does your team type per month, and what else could those hours do?

## Never say
Grand AI-transformation narratives to this audience; accuracy numbers without a pilot

## Validation needed
Pilot on their documents; gates (edition, pgvector, IAP) checked
