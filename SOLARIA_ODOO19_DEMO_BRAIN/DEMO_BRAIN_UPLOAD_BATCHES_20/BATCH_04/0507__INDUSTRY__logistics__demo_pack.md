# Industry Demo Pack: Logistics

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | INDUSTRY (defaults to confirm; never a substitute for client intake) |
| Product truth | All module claims route via the Intelligence Pack (foundation 0012); editions labelled; statutory items always validation |

## Operating model
Move and store goods for others or as the backbone of own trade; margin lives in space, touches and transport optimisation.

## Common pain points
- Paper-driven warehouse floors
- No real-time stock truth
- Carrier and freight cost opacity
- Customer-specific handling rules in heads

## Common process flows
- Inbound receiving and putaway
- Storage and internal moves
- Outbound wave/batch picking
- Carrier handoff
- Cycle counting

## Relevant Odoo modules
- stock with routes and locations (Community core)
- stock_barcode, quality checks, carrier connectors (Enterprise, labelled); honest boundary to full 3PL/WMS suites

## Relevant KPIs
Dock-to-stock, pick rate, inventory accuracy, cost per order shipped, space utilisation

## Suitable demo storyline
Warehouse-to-Delivery spine: receive against ASN-like PO, putaway by rules, pick with scanner, pack, carrier label, proof of delivery trail (storyline 2050)

## Persona emphasis
Warehouse managers, COO, IT architect for integrations, operators as end users

## Challenger insights
- Real-time stock is a scanning-moment discipline, not a report
- Every unscanned touch is invisible cost; the floor should write the books

## Likely objections
- We might need a real WMS (honest boundary: execution yes, optimisation algorithms no; one execution owner rule)
- 3PL billing? (custom/integration territory, flagged honestly)

## Data required for demo
Realistic location tree, GS1-style barcodes on hero SKUs, one inbound and one outbound staged, a cycle count, carrier setup labelled

## Localisation and validation concerns
- Customs/bonded flows out of standard scope (flag for design)
- Device fleet and wifi survey (validation)

## What not to overclaim
- Wave/slotting optimisation
- Yard management
- 3PL client billing as standard
