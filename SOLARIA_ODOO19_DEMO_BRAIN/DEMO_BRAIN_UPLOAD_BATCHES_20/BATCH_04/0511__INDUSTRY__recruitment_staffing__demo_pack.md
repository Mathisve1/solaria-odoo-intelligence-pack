# Industry Demo Pack: Recruitment and Staffing

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | INDUSTRY (defaults to confirm; never a substitute for client intake) |
| Product truth | All module claims route via the Intelligence Pack (foundation 0012); editions labelled; statutory items always validation |

## Operating model
Match talent to demand fast; margin lives in fill rate, speed, and consultant productivity; compliance around candidate data is constant.

## Common pain points
- CV chaos across inboxes
- Slow candidate response losing placements
- No pipeline truth per role
- GDPR anxiety
- Client and candidate sides in different tools

## Common process flows
- Job intake to sourcing to shortlist
- Interview loops
- Offer and placement
- Timesheet-to-bill for temp staffing
- Talent pooling

## Relevant Odoo modules
- hr_recruitment with talent pools, website jobs (Community)
- CV parsing OCR and recruitment AI assist (Enterprise, IAP, human-decision governance always), sign for contracts, timesheets for temp billing

## Relevant KPIs
Time-to-fill, fill rate, candidate response time, placements per consultant, pool reuse rate

## Suitable demo storyline
Recruit-to-Hire spine: job published, application flows in, CV parsed (Enterprise, labelled, human decision shown), interview loop, offer signed digitally, candidate becomes placement; talent-pool save as the second-chance beat (storyline 2090)

## Persona emphasis
Recruitment director as sales director pattern, consultants as end users, compliance owner, CFO for temp billing

## Challenger insights
- Speed wins placements: the first credible shortlist frames the search
- A talent pool with retention automation is an asset; a CV graveyard is a liability

## Likely objections
- GDPR and candidate data (governance-first answer: retention automation, access design)
- AI screening fears (human decides, EU AI Act framing, never autonomous)
- Our job boards? (integration check per board)

## Data required for demo
Open positions, applicant pipeline staged across stages, sample CVs tested with parsing same-day, refusal templates, one offer-to-sign case

## Localisation and validation concerns
- Candidate data retention rules per country (legal validation)
- Temp-staffing payroll boundary (out of demo scope, flagged)

## What not to overclaim
- Screening accuracy numbers
- Autonomous candidate ranking
- Job-board coverage without per-board checks
