# Industry Demo Pack: Automotive (suppliers and services)

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | INDUSTRY (defaults to confirm; never a substitute for client intake) |
| Product truth | All module claims route via the Intelligence Pack (foundation 0012); editions labelled; statutory items always validation |

## Operating model
Precision supply chains with OEM-grade expectations: EDI schedules, PPAP-like quality culture, sequenced deliveries; or dealer/service operations.

## Common pain points
- OEM EDI expectations
- Quality documentation burden
- Traceability to the component
- Schedule volatility
- Aftermarket parts logistics

## Common process flows
- Schedule-driven order-to-cash
- Plan-to-produce with quality gates
- Traceability chains
- Service/aftermarket parts flows

## Relevant Odoo modules
- mrp, stock, purchase, sale (Community core)
- quality, shop floor, barcode (Enterprise, labelled); EDI per network is integration territory (validated per case)

## Relevant KPIs
PPM defects, OTD to schedule, traceability response time, schedule adherence, inventory turns

## Suitable demo storyline
Plan-to-Produce with quality and traceability emphasis; the serial-number journey from component receipt to shipped assembly as the crescendo

## Persona emphasis
Quality manager, plant manager, supply chain lead, CFO

## Challenger insights
- Audit readiness is a daily-capture by-product, not a documentation sprint
- Schedule volatility hurts most where visibility is weakest: at component availability

## Likely objections
- OEM EDI networks? (integration per network, validated, never assumed)
- IATF-style requirements? (records support audits; certification mapping is expert design work)

## Data required for demo
Serial/lot-tracked hero assembly, quality points with one engineered failure, receipt-to-delivery trace prepared, schedule-like recurring orders

## Localisation and validation concerns
- EDI network specifics (validation per OEM)
- Quality standard mapping (expert validation)

## What not to overclaim
- Native OEM EDI coverage
- IATF compliance from module existence
- Sequenced JIT delivery orchestration as standard
