# Industry Demo Pack: Construction

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | INDUSTRY (defaults to confirm; never a substitute for client intake) |
| Product truth | All module claims route via the Intelligence Pack (foundation 0012); editions labelled; statutory items always validation |

## Operating model
Project-driven delivery with heavy subcontracting and materials logistics; margin lives in estimation accuracy, progress billing and claims control.

## Common pain points
- Estimation to execution disconnect
- Progress billing disputes
- Material logistics to sites
- Subcontractor coordination
- Document and approval chaos

## Common process flows
- Tender to project
- Progress/milestone billing
- Procure-to-site materials
- Site timesheets
- Document approval trails

## Relevant Odoo modules
- project, sale, purchase, stock, hr_timesheet (Community)
- documents, approvals, sign, planning, field service for site interventions (Enterprise, labelled)

## Relevant KPIs
Margin per project phase, billed versus complete, subcontractor cost variance, document turnaround, site productivity

## Suitable demo storyline
Project-to-Invoice with a construction accent: quote as phased budget, materials procured to the site, site hours logged, progress invoice, document approval beat (storyline 2060 adapted)

## Persona emphasis
Project directors, CFO, site managers as operations managers, procurement lead

## Challenger insights
- Progress billing disputes are documentation problems from week one, not negotiation problems at month nine
- Site chaos is usually a materials-information gap, not a people gap

## Likely objections
- Industry-specific needs (honest: configuration plus validation workshop; no vertical-magic claims)
- Our subcontracting flows (purchase plus project patterns, validated)

## Data required for demo
Phased project with budget lines, purchase to a site location, site timesheets, one progress invoice, one document approval chain

## Localisation and validation concerns
- Progress billing and retention rules per country (finance validation)
- Payroll/works regulations out of demo scope, flagged

## What not to overclaim
- Full construction ERP verticals (Odoo covers the core; industry depth is configuration and design)
- WBS/planning depth of specialist tools
