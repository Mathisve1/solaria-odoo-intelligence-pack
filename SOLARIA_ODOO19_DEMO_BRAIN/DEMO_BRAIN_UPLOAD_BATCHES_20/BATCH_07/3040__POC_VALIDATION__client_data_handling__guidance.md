# Client Data Handling Guidance (demos and POCs)

Category: POC_VALIDATION. Trust is won in demos and lost in data incidents.

## Principles
1. Minimum necessary data: request the smallest sample that proves the claim (one month of bills, twenty quotes, one BoM family).
2. Anonymise by default for demo use: real structures, fictional names, unless the client explicitly approves live data in writing.
3. Written handling terms BEFORE receiving anything: storage location, access list, retention date, destruction confirmation.
4. Client data never enters shared demo databases used for other prospects; one client, one environment.
5. AI/OCR processing of client documents counts as data processing: confirm the data-boundary stance from intake 0280 (what may leave their environment) and the IAP implication explicitly.
6. Deloitte information-security policies prevail over anything in this pack; when in doubt, ask the risk office before receiving data.

## Practical flow
Request (minimal, listed) > handling terms confirmed > receive via approved channel (never personal email) > log receipt in the POC register > use within scope > destroy on the agreed date > confirm destruction in writing.

## Red lines
No production credentials from the client, ever, in a pre-sales context. No personal data beyond what the claim strictly needs (CV parsing pilots: obtain consent posture from the client, prefer their anonymised or expired-consent-cleared samples). No screenshots of client data in follow-up materials without written approval.
