# Executive Demos and the Process-Led Method

Extends foundations 0008, 0009, 0014. Category: DEMO_METHOD.

## Executive demo method (30 minutes that decide sponsorship)
- Structure: context playback (2 min), challenge + insight (5), ONE continuous flow (12), management view with one drill (5), implementation realism (2), close + next step (4).
- Language: outcomes and money; zero settings screens; module names only when asked.
- The one-flow rule: executives remember one story told completely, not three told partially.
- Prepared depth: keep functional detail one click away for the unexpected operational question, but never volunteer it.

## The process-led method (all formats)
- Choose the spine object (0009) and introduce it like a character with a name.
- Apps are stations, not chapters: never announce app names as agenda items.
- Chapter for mixed audiences: label chapters by business phase (sell it, fulfil it, get paid), not by module.
- The camera rule: the screen follows the object; if a click does not advance the object's journey, cut it.

## Storytelling patterns that work in ERP demos
- Day-in-the-life: one persona's morning, before versus after.
- The order with a problem: the exception beat carries more conviction than ten happy paths.
- The invisible accountant: operations happen, books write themselves; reveal the journal entry at the end.
- Before/after timer: the same task timed in the old way (told) and the new way (shown).

## What kills executive demos
Menu tours, more than one flow, unlabelled Enterprise moments, configuration detours, answering operational questions at length, running over into the Q&A budget.
