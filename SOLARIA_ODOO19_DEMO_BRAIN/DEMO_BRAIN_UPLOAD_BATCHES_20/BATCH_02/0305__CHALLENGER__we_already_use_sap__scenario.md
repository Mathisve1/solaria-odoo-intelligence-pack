# Challenger Scenario: We already use SAP

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Client (or a division) runs SAP; Odoo is challenged as a downgrade or an odd fit.

## Underlying concern
Safety in the incumbent; fear of being the person who chose the smaller vendor.

## Challenger insight
The question is fit-to-segment, not brand hierarchy: mid-market entities inside SAP groups often pay tier-1 process overhead for tier-3 needs, in licences, in change-request queues, in every small adjustment.

## Reframe
Not 'SAP versus Odoo' but 'which entities and processes are over-served, and what does that overhead cost per year'.

## Recommended response pattern
- Acknowledge SAP's strength at group scale
- position two-tier honestly: group core stays, subsidiaries and edge processes run lighter
- offer the fit-assessment rather than a religion debate

## Demo action
Show speed-to-change: a stage, an approval threshold and a report changed live in minutes (configuration, labelled)

## Advancing question
Which of your entities or processes feels most over-served by the group template today?

## Never say
Anything mocking SAP; unverifiable cost claims; feature-parity promises

## Validation needed
Actual licensing/TCO comparisons are commercial exercises with real numbers, never demo claims
