# Quick Intake Form (the starred minimum)

Ten questions that usually reach completeness band 3 (foundation 0004). Category: CLIENT_INTAKE.

1. Client, industry, country: ______
2. Company size (people, entities): ______  Current ERP: ______
3. Sales stage and meeting type: ______
4. Date, duration, format, language: ______
5. Who is in the room (roles) and who decides: ______
6. Top two business challenges, with how the process runs today: ______
7. What must the audience understand / believe / feel afterwards: ______
8. In-scope processes; explicit out-of-scope: ______
9. The one wow moment the client themselves asked for: ______
10. Landmines: sensitive topics, set expectations, known objections: ______

Rule: answers here are client facts; anything still blank becomes a labelled assumption or a question back (never a silent guess).
