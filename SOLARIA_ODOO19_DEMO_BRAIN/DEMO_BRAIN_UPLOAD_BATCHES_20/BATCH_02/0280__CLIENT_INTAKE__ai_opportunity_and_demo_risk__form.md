# AI Opportunity and Demo-Risk Intake

Two short forms that prevent the two classic demo failures: AI overreach and surprise landmines. Category: CLIENT_INTAKE.

## AI opportunity intake
- What does the client imagine when they say AI (their words): ______
- Document volumes: vendor bills/month, CVs/month, expense receipts, inbound docs: ______
- Appetite versus fear: who wants it, who fears it, works council involved: ______
- Data boundary stance (what may leave their environment): ______
- Prior AI experiments and their outcomes: ______
- Edition/hosting reality check: Enterprise? pgvector-capable hosting? IAP budget? (gates, validated): ______
Rule: AI beats enter the storyline only with same-day rehearsal and the governance framing (module pack 1230 rules).

## Demo-risk intake
- Which requirements are still unclear (each becomes an assumption or a question): ______
- Which data can we NOT show realistically (drives storyline choices): ______
- Which functionality do WE consider unstable for live demo (backup plans per 0016): ______
- Which expectations were set by others (previous partner, competitor, internal champion): ______
- Sensitive commercial topics and the agreed deflection: ______
- Time available for preparation, honestly, in person-days: ______

Output: the risk section of the demo plan (output contract section 15) and the backup flow design (section 16).
