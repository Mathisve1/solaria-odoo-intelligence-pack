# Demo Preparation Lifecycle (practice-level guide)

Extends foundation 0005 with a working calendar. Category: DEMO_METHOD.

## T-minus schedule (standard demo, adjust to reality)
| When | What | Owner | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-10 days | Intake complete or gaps escalated (0003/0004) | Opportunity owner | Score >= 60 |
| T-8 | Challenge diagnosis + audience map (0006/0008) | Pre-sales lead | Top 2 challenges at root cause |
| T-7 | Scope mapping + ladder classification (0012/0010) | Solution architect | Catalog-verified, editions tagged |
| T-6 | Storyline draft (0009) + timing check (0014) | Pre-sales lead | Within format limits |
| T-5 | Runbook + Challenger sheet + objection sheet | Team | Owners per beat |
| T-4 | Demo data built (0016) | Environment owner | Checklist green |
| T-3 | First full rehearsal (0017) | All presenters | Beats pass or get cut |
| T-2 | Fixes + second rehearsal of weak beats | All | All beats demo-validated |
| T-1 | Environment freeze, reset executed, tabs prepared | Environment owner | Reset proven |
| T-0 | Session + capture | All | Parking log kept |
| T+1 | Follow-up pack out; registers updated (0018) | Opportunity owner | Every claim has an owner |

## Roles (small team pattern)
Opportunity owner (client truth, intake), pre-sales lead (storyline, room control), solution architect (scope honesty, annex), environment owner (data, reset, backups). One person may hold two roles; no role may be unheld.

## The three meetings that make demos good
1. Diagnosis session (internal, 45 min): challenges to root cause, insight drafting.
2. Storyline walkthrough (internal, 30 min): kill orphan wow moments, timing sanity.
3. Rehearsal (full dress): the gate that decides what the client sees.

## Failure smells
Intake still open at T-5; data built after rehearsal; storyline written by one person the night before; no named environment owner.
