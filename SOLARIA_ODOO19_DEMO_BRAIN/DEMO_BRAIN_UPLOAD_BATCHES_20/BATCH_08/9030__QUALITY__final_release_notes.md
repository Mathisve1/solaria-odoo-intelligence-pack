# Demo Brain Final Release Notes (v1.0)

Category: QUALITY (meta artifact; not product evidence).

## What this release contains
The first specialised context brain built on the Odoo 19.0 Solaria Intelligence Pack: the **Deloitte Odoo 19.0 Demo & Pre-Sales Brain**. It converts the general product-knowledge pack into a working pre-sales advisor with a constitutional first-20 foundation, deep supporting layers, and a gated upload procedure.

## Contents by layer
- **Foundation (20 files, 0000-0019):** identity and read-me, paste-ready global context, non-negotiable rules, the mandatory intake form with 0-100 completeness scoring, the 14-stage preparation workflow, challenge diagnosis, Challenger methodology, audience adaptation (16-persona matrix), the 12-beat process-led storyline framework, the 7-level standard-before-custom demo ladder, Community/Enterprise demo discipline, product-knowledge routing into the existing pack, the 18-section output contract, timing and scope limits per format, the objection framework, demo data and environment requirements, the validation and rehearsal checklist, the 9-label claim-control vocabulary, and the machine-readable foundation manifest.
- **Supporting layers (121 files):** 6 demo-method guides, 11 intake forms (md + json), 13 challenger files (library, template, 11 scenarios), 16 persona packs, 12 industry packs, 24 module demo packs, 15 storylines, 6 POC/validation frameworks, 13 output templates, 2 objection-library files, acceptance and red-team test suites, and the operator guide.
- **Quality layer:** this file, the quality control report, the foundation consistency audit, the file-type compliance report, and the packaging report in the flat folder.
- **Upload artifacts:** `DEMO_BRAIN_FLAT_UPLOAD_ALL/` (single folder, no subfolders, sortable names) and `DEMO_BRAIN_UPLOAD_BATCHES_20/` (BATCH_01 = exactly the 20 foundation files; later batches max 20 files each).

## Design decisions worth knowing
1. **First-20 weighting respected:** the foundation carries permanent principles only; deep detail lives in later ranges; later files declare that foundations win on conflict.
2. **Product truth stays in the Intelligence Pack:** this brain routes rather than duplicates; the module catalog remains the only existence authority; the edition map remains the edition authority.
3. **Intake-first is enforced, not suggested:** completeness scoring gates output depth; the red-team suite attacks exactly this behaviour.
4. **Everything client-facing is label-disciplined:** editions, ladder levels and the nine claim labels appear throughout the supporting layers, not only in the rules.
5. **No em dashes anywhere**, per user style preference (verified by sweep).

## Known limitations
Defaults-to-confirm nature of persona/industry packs; high-level competitor posture by design; dependency on the product pack for module facts; retrieval behaviour verifiable only after upload (that is what the test suites are for).

## Next-release candidates
Additional industry packs on demand (from real pursuit logs); storyline variants per industry; a Dutch/French language layer for client-facing templates; persona packs for procurement and works-council roles; scenario coverage for two-tier SAP conversations in more depth.
