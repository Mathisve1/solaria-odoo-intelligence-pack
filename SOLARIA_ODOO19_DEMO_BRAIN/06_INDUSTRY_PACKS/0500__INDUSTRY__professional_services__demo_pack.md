## Operating model
Sell expertise by the hour, day or deliverable; margin lives in utilisation, rates and leakage control; people are the inventory.

## Common pain points
- Unbilled hours and scope creep
- Margin visible only after project end
- Timesheet friction and late entries
- Bench and staffing blindness
- Proposal-to-project handoff losing information

## Common process flows
- Lead to proposal to engagement
- Sold hours to staffed project to timesheets to invoice
- Milestone or retainer billing cycles
- Utilisation and capacity planning

## Relevant Odoo modules
- crm, sale, project, hr_timesheet, sale_project (Community)
- timesheet_grid validation, planning/forecast, documents, sign, subscriptions for retainers (Enterprise, labelled)

## Relevant KPIs
Utilisation, realised rate, project margin, unbilled hours, timesheet compliance, DSO

## Suitable demo storyline
Project-to-Invoice as the spine: quote with service lines, project auto-created, hours logged from the task, milestone invoiced, margin viewed live (storyline 2060 pattern)

## Persona emphasis
Managing partner as CEO pattern, finance director, project managers, consultants as end users

## Challenger insights
- Leakage beats rates: most firms chase rate increases while losing points to unbilled scope
- Margin monthly, not annually, changes staffing decisions while they still matter

## Likely objections
- Our billing models are special (rebuild one real contract)
- Consultants will not timesheet (two-minute capture shown)
- We live in spreadsheets (live margin view as the counter)

## Data required for demo
Service products with rates, one flagship project with phases, 3 weeks of timesheets across 4 consultants, one milestone invoice, utilisation-ready history

## Localisation and validation concerns
Country invoicing rules, VAT on services cross-border, timesheet-to-payroll boundary (validation), retainer revenue recognition (finance validation)

## What not to overclaim
- Resource optimisation algorithms (planning is assisted, not an optimizer)
- Automatic revenue recognition without finance design
