# Industry Demo Pack: Field Services

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | INDUSTRY (defaults to confirm; never a substitute for client intake) |
| Product truth | All module claims route via the Intelligence Pack (foundation 0012); editions labelled; statutory items always validation |

## Operating model
Revenue earned at the customer's site: installations, maintenance, repairs; margin lives in first-time-fix, utilisation, and billing every intervention.

## Common pain points
- Paper worksheets
- Interventions that never get invoiced
- Dispatch by phone and memory
- Parts vans as black holes
- Proof-of-service disputes

## Common process flows
- Ticket-to-intervention
- Plan and dispatch
- Execute on site with materials
- Sign-off and invoice on site
- Preventive maintenance cycles

## Relevant Odoo modules
- industry_fsm on the Enterprise project/timesheet layer, helpdesk intake, planning (all Enterprise, labelled)
- stock for van inventory, sale for service products (Community core)

## Relevant KPIs
First-time-fix, interventions per tech per day, unbilled intervention rate, travel ratio, parts availability on van

## Suitable demo storyline
Field-Service-to-Invoice on a phone: ticket escalates, dispatcher assigns on the map, technician timer, worksheet, signature, invoice before leaving the driveway (storyline 2080)

## Persona emphasis
Service director as COO pattern, dispatchers, technicians as end users, CFO for the unbilled-work story

## Challenger insights
- The most expensive intervention is the one you never invoice; capture at the doorstep beats reconstruction at the office
- First-time-fix is a parts-information problem more than a skills problem

## Likely objections
- Offline in the field? (validate the envelope on devices, honestly)
- Route optimisation? (assisted planning yes, algorithmic optimisation is integration territory)
- Device costs?

## Data required for demo
Technician users on mobile, service products, one van stock, a ticket pre-staged, worksheet template for the intervention type, customer for signature

## Localisation and validation concerns
- Working-time regulations for technicians (out of demo scope, flag)
- Device and connectivity pilot (validation)

## What not to overclaim
- Route optimisation
- Offline guarantees without site testing
- Community field-service (the app layer is Enterprise)
