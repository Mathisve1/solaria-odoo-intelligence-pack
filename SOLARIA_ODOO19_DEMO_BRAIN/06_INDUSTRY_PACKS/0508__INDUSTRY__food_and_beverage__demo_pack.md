# Industry Demo Pack: Food and Beverage

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | INDUSTRY (defaults to confirm; never a substitute for client intake) |
| Product truth | All module claims route via the Intelligence Pack (foundation 0012); editions labelled; statutory items always validation |

## Operating model
Produce and distribute perishable goods under traceability obligations; margin lives in yield, waste, and recall-proof processes.

## Common pain points
- Lot traceability under audit pressure
- Expiry-driven waste
- Recall fear
- Quality documentation effort
- Seasonal planning

## Common process flows
- Plan-to-produce with lots and expiry
- Quality gates on receipt and production
- FEFO picking
- Recall simulation
- Catch-weight-like specifics (validation)

## Relevant Odoo modules
- mrp, stock with lots and expiry (Community core, product_expiry)
- quality, shop floor, barcode (Enterprise, labelled)

## Relevant KPIs
Yield, waste percentage, recall trace time, quality check pass rate, OTIF

## Suitable demo storyline
Plan-to-Produce with a traceability crescendo: produce with lot capture, quality gate, FEFO pick, then the recall drill: one lot traced both directions in two minutes (storyline 2040 adapted)

## Persona emphasis
Quality manager, production manager, COO, compliance-minded CFO

## Challenger insights
- Recall readiness is cheaper as a by-product of daily scanning than as an annual project
- Waste is a FEFO-discipline number more than a forecasting number

## Likely objections
- Industry compliance (IFS/BRC-style)? (honest: evidence and records support audits; certification mapping is design work with experts)
- Catch weight? (validation workshop before any claim)

## Data required for demo
Lot-tracked components and finished goods with expiry dates, quality points, one lot spanning purchase to delivery for the recall drill

## Localisation and validation concerns
- Food-law specifics per country (expert validation)
- Process-manufacturing behaviours (dedicated validation workshop)

## What not to overclaim
- Certification compliance out of the box
- Catch-weight economics
- LIMS-depth lab management
