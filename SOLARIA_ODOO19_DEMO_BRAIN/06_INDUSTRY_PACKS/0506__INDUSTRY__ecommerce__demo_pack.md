# Industry Demo Pack: eCommerce

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | INDUSTRY (defaults to confirm; never a substitute for client intake) |
| Product truth | All module claims route via the Intelligence Pack (foundation 0012); editions labelled; statutory items always validation |

## Operating model
Digital storefront with fulfilment economics; margin lives in conversion, basket size, fulfilment cost per order and returns control.

## Common pain points
- Shop and ERP as separate worlds
- Content and stock drift
- Returns eating margin quietly
- Carrier costs opaque
- Abandoned revenue

## Common process flows
- Website-to-order to fulfilment
- Returns and refunds
- Catalog and content management
- Promotions and cross-sell
- Payment reconciliation

## Relevant Odoo modules
- website_sale, stock, payment providers, loyalty (Community)
- carrier connectors at checkout, marketing automation, WhatsApp notifications (Enterprise, labelled)

## Relevant KPIs
Conversion, average order value, fulfilment cost per order, return rate, payment reconciliation effort, abandoned-cart recovery

## Suitable demo storyline
Website-to-Order spine: cart to payment to automatic delivery order and invoice, then the ops view: pick, ship, reconcile; abandoned-cart recovery as the growth beat (storyline 2110)

## Persona emphasis
eCommerce manager, COO for fulfilment, CFO for reconciliation, marketing lead

## Challenger insights
- The integration tax between shop and ERP is paid on every order; zero-interface commerce changes the cost curve
- Returns policy is a margin lever masquerading as a service question

## Likely objections
- Performance at our traffic? (hosting architecture validation, honest)
- Our PSP and carriers? (connector checks per provider)
- SEO migration risk? (redirect plan as project work)

## Data required for demo
Webshop with client-flavoured catalog, customer account with saved details, payment sandbox, one return case, populated sales dashboard

## Localisation and validation concerns
OSS/VAT display rules per market (validation), PSP contracts (commercial), carrier coverage (per connector)

## What not to overclaim
- Hyperscale readiness
- Marketplace-grade multi-vendor
- PIM-depth content management
