# Time Recovery and Room Control

Extends foundation 0014. Category: DEMO_METHOD.

## When the demo runs late
1. Compress the pre-marked accordion beats (0014) to one sentence each.
2. Cut reporting depth to a single drill; cut the AI beat unless AI is a stated objective.
3. Never cut: client-context playback, the exception beat if operations people are present, the closing (takeaways + next step), delivered in its rehearsed 90-second form if needed.
4. Announce the adjustment confidently: 'To respect your time I will fast-forward the middle of the journey and protect the part you came for.' Control communicated is competence.

## When the room derails you
- The deep-dive hijacker: 'Great question; it deserves better than a rushed answer: parking it for the follow-up with an owner.' Log visibly.
- The sceptic performing: acknowledge the concern category (0015), give the honest one-liner, offer the dedicated session; never debate for the room's entertainment.
- The side conversation: pause, name the topic ('you are on pricing, correct?'), either fold it in via its objection entry or park it.
- The early-leaving decision maker: deliver the 90-second close TO THEM before they leave, then continue for the room.

## When technology fails
One retry, then backup (0016/0017 rule): switch to the twin record, or the rehearsal recording, narrating forward. Never debug live beyond sixty seconds; never apologise twice for the same failure.

## When you do not know
The honesty reflex (0018): 'I will not guess on this; it goes on the validation list with an owner and you will have the answer by Friday.' Said once, confidently, it builds more trust than three fluent improvisations.
