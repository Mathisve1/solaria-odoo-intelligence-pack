# Conversions: Discovery-to-Demo, Demo-to-POC, and Follow-Up

Extends foundation 0005 stages 1, 13, 14. Category: DEMO_METHOD.

## Discovery-to-demo conversion
- Discovery output IS demo input: run discovery against the intake form (0003) so nothing is asked twice.
- Convert findings into the challenge table (0006) within 48 hours while nuance is fresh.
- Close discovery with the demo contract: 'Based on today, the demo will prove A, B, C on your vocabulary; correct us now.'
- Do not demo in the discovery meeting beyond one teaser moment; hunger is an asset.

## Demo-to-POC conversion
- Trigger: demo went well AND open claims need proof on client data (volume, pricing cases, OCR rates, integrations).
- The pitch pattern: 'You saw it on our data; the POC proves it on yours, against success criteria we sign together.'
- Never a free pilot without scope: use the POC qualification framework (3000 range) to decide if a POC is even warranted.
- POC scope comes from the validation register (0018): the open claims ARE the POC content.

## Follow-up (T+1, non-negotiable)
Contents of the follow-up pack: thank-you note with the three takeaways in client words; answers to parked questions (or owners and dates); the validation register extract relevant to them; the proposed next step with calendar options; edition legend if any Enterprise moments were shown.

## Multi-session strategy
When one demo cannot serve all audiences (0008 rule): sequence sessions (executive 30, functional 60 per domain, technical annex 60), each with its own storyline instantiation, one shared spine customer across all sessions for continuity.

## The register rhythm
After every client touch: update assumption and validation registers (0018), re-score intake (0004), adjust the next session's storyline. Preparation is a loop, not a line.
