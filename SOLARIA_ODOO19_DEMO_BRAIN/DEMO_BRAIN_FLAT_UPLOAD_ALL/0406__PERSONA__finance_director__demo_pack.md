## Priorities
- Clean books with less manual effort
- AP/AR productivity
- Statutory obligations met without heroics

## Likely frustrations
- Vendor bill typing
- Chasing approvals by email
- Reconciliation backlogs

## Business KPIs
Cost per invoice processed, unreconciled items age, on-time filing, AR overdue percentage

## Demo emphasis
- The AP arc: emailed PDF bill, OCR fills it (Enterprise, IAP, labelled), validation queue, PO match, payment run
- Bank reconciliation with auto-match assistance (Enterprise)
- Dunning flow

## Relevant Odoo modules
account (Community), account_accountant, account_reports, invoice extract, batch payments and SEPA (Enterprise, labelled per country)

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Touchless AP fails on exception design, not on OCR quality; the validation queue is where the productivity lives
- AR ageing is a process cadence problem the system can enforce

## Likely objections
- Our country specifics? (localisation validation)
- OCR accuracy promises? (pilot on their documents, never a number upfront)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Sales-heavy storylines
- AI hype without the validation queue shown

## Opening questions
- How many vendor bills per month, and how long does one take end to end?
- Which filing deadline stresses your team most?

## Recommended closing
Send us one month of anonymised vendor bills; the POC measures your touchless rate on your own paper.
