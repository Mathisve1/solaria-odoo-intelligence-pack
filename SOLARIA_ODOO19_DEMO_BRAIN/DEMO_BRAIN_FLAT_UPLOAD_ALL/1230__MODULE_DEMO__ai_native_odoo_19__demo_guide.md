## Demo purpose
AI in the flow of work, honestly: OCR that fills documents, assistants with a review step, all labelled and rehearsed.

## Best personas
CEO ('wants AI'), CFO (AP), HR (CV parsing), CIO (governance)

## Prerequisites
- Enterprise database with AI/pgvector available (validated!)
- IAP credits
- same-day rehearsal of EVERY ai moment
- fallback recording

## Minimum demo data
- Real-ish vendor bills tested today
- a messy documents inbox staged
- CV samples tested today if recruitment in scope

## Recommended flow
- PDF bill to filled bill with the validation queue as the hero
- documents auto-sorted with review shown
- one drafting assist
- then the concept slide, labelled Deloitte concept

## Wow moments
- Bill filled in seconds WITH the human check shown
- the honest 'what does not exist natively' moment (credibility spike)

## Common mistakes
- First-run AI live (never)
- accuracy numbers
- concepts blurred into product
- Community clients shown AI without the gate stated

## Standard vs custom notes
- Native modules: configuration-level activation with governance
- Company-Brain-class solutions: Deloitte concept/custom, introduced as such BEFORE showing

## Community vs Enterprise notes
The entire native AI layer is Enterprise-only, with pgvector and IAP gates: three facts, always stated

## Likely objections
- How accurate is it (pilot on client data, never a number)
- data boundaries (contractual validation)
- AI risk (governance doc framing)

## Validation checklist
- Gates checked (edition, pgvector, IAP)
- same-day rehearsal log
- governance/legal review done for the audience

## Backup flow
Recording of the same flow from rehearsal; deterministic path continues the storyline if AI misbehaves
