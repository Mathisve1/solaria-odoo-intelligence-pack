## Demo purpose
Procedures living next to the work: articles with live data inside, the curated brain of the company.

## Best personas
Operations, support leads, HR, anyone drowning in wikis

## Prerequisites
- Enterprise database
- article tree with client-flavoured content
- one article embedding a live view

## Minimum demo data
- 10 articles in a sensible hierarchy
- one SOP embedding a live task/kanban view
- permissions set for the sharing beat

## Recommended flow
- Find the SOP
- the live embedded view moment
- edit collaboratively
- share with member permissions
- helpdesk agent inserts an article answer

## Wow moments
- A procedure document with the actual live queue inside it
- answer-insertion in a ticket

## Common mistakes
- Wiki-feature-war framing
- ignoring ownership/curation honesty
- overclaiming AI answering without governance

## Standard vs custom notes
- Structure, templates, permissions: configuration
- AI grounding on articles: Enterprise, piloted with governance

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Enterprise-only; the AI-grounding angle raises stakes on content quality (advisory hook)

## Likely objections
- We have Confluence (coexistence boundary: process-adjacent knowledge here)
- who maintains it (ownership honesty)

## Validation checklist
- Taxonomy and owners named
- permission matrix tested
- AI answer pilot criteria if in scope

## Backup flow
Second article path prepared; embed pre-rendered
