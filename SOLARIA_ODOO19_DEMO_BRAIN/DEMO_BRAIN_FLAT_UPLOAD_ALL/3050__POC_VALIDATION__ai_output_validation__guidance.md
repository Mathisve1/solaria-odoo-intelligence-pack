# AI Output Validation Guidance (pre-sales scope)

Category: POC_VALIDATION. Applies module pack 1230 and the product pack's AI governance; this is the pre-sales operational version.

## Before any AI moment reaches a client
1. Gates verified: Enterprise edition, pgvector-capable hosting, IAP credits (all three, explicitly).
2. Same-day rehearsal on the exact documents that will be used live; outputs reviewed by a human presenter.
3. Fallback prepared: recording of the successful rehearsal plus the deterministic continuation of the storyline.
4. Labelling ready: which sentence introduces the moment (edition, and 'assisted, human validates' framing).

## In POCs (AI claims under proof)
- Two-part criteria always (3010): the capability ran on client data AND the quality threshold agreed at kickoff was met.
- Sample discipline: agree the document sample (volume, period, languages) before anyone sees results.
- Measure honestly: touchless rate, correction rate, and time-per-document; report all three, not the flattering one.
- Human-in-the-loop is the design, not the disclaimer: show the validation queue as the workflow.

## Phrasing rules (from the product pack's AI governance, non-negotiable)
Never quote accuracy numbers that did not come from THIS client's pilot. Never present Deloitte AI concepts as product. Never let 'the AI will handle it' pass unchallenged in the room: correct to 'the AI drafts, your team confirms'.

## When AI output is wrong live
One calm sentence: 'Good, you saw the review step catch it: that queue is exactly the control your auditors will ask about.' Then continue. A caught error, well handled, converts sceptics better than a perfect run.
