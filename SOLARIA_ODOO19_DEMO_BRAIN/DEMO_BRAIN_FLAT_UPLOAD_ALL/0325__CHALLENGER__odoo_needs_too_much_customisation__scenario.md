# Challenger Scenario: Odoo requires too much customisation

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
A past experience or competitor whisper says Odoo projects drown in custom code.

## Underlying concern
Upgrade debt, dependency on developers, project overruns.

## Challenger insight
Customisation volume is a governance choice, not a product property: ungoverned projects customise any platform to death. The ladder (standard, configuration, Studio, automation, custom) exists precisely to keep builds rare and owned.

## Reframe
The right question is 'what discipline will our partner enforce', not 'how customisable is the product'.

## Recommended response pattern
- Agree that over-customisation is the classic failure mode
- present the standard-before-custom contract behaviour (challenge sessions, custom registry, gates)
- show configuration answering a request they assumed needed code

## Demo action
Take one of their 'we would need custom' requirements and resolve it live via configuration or Studio (labelled)

## Advancing question
Bring your three scariest requirements to the workshop: if two of three land on configuration, does the risk picture change?

## Never say
'You will never need custom code' (dishonest); blaming their previous integrator by name

## Validation needed
The three requirements walked on the ladder with evidence, in a working session
