## Demo purpose
The schedule as a living Gantt: plan, publish, let people self-serve open shifts.

## Best personas
Operations managers, services/field leads, HR-adjacent planners

## Prerequisites
- Enterprise database
- roles and working calendars set
- employees with avatars (it demos better)

## Minimum demo data
- Two weeks planned for a pilot team
- shift templates
- one open shift staged

## Recommended flow
- Copy last week
- drag adjustments
- publish (people notified)
- employee self-assigns the open shift

## Wow moments
- Copy-week in one gesture
- the publish moment
- self-assignment landing live

## Common mistakes
- Calling it workforce-management (labor-law engines are not in scope)
- planning strangers with no names

## Standard vs custom notes
- Templates, roles, publishing: configuration
- optimization/rostering algorithms: integration boundary, honest

## Community vs Enterprise notes
Enterprise-only; say it plainly, with the Community fallback honesty (no scheduling app there)

## Likely objections
- Union/labor rules (boundary: calendars and process, compliance engines external)
- swap flows (validate live)

## Validation checklist
- Calendars/roles for the pilot team
- publication policy
- planned-vs-actual reporting needs

## Backup flow
Second week pre-planned; screenshots of the mobile view
