## Priorities
- Operability: users, rights, backups, upgrades
- Not being left alone with a black box
- Sane change management

## Likely frustrations
- Admin tasks that need a consultant
- Upgrade fear
- Undocumented changes by enthusiastic key users

## Business KPIs
Time per user change, incident resolution time, upgrade downtime, audit findings on access

## Demo emphasis
- The admin surfaces honestly: user management, groups, the customisation registry idea
- What a key user may do (Studio governance, Enterprise) versus what stays with admins
- Where logs and trails live

## Relevant Odoo modules
Settings/users/groups surfaces, web_studio governance (Enterprise, labelled), scheduled actions visibility

## Suitable Challenger insights
- The dangerous customisation is the unregistered one; governance makes key-user autonomy safe instead of scary
- Upgrade pain is proportional to undocumented change

## Likely objections
- Who supports us after go-live? (support model honestly)
- Can we undo what key users build? (staging and export story)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Pure business narrative with no operability content
- Glossing over maintenance reality

## Opening questions
- What breaks when your current admin goes on holiday?
- How do changes reach production today?

## Recommended closing
We include the operations handbook and governance registry in the proposal; you should never be alone with this system.
