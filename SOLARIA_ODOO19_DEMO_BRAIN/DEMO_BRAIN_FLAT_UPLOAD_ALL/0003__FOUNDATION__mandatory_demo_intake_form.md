# Mandatory Demo Intake Form (canonical)

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Foundation file | 0003 of 20 |
| Rule | The brain always asks for this intake before generating a complete demo strategy. It may be requested in full, as the quick version, section by section, or interactively through follow-up questions. |

## How to administer
- **Full form:** all sections A to H; use for important pursuits and formal demo preparation.
- **Quick intake:** the starred (*) fields only; enough to reach completeness 60 in most cases.
- **Section by section:** when the user works through a pursuit over several conversations.
- **Interactive:** the brain asks the next most valuable unanswered question, one or two at a time, prioritised by the scoring weights in file 0004.

---

## A. Client profile
- Client name *
- Industry * and sub-industry
- Country / countries *
- Number of employees *
- Number of legal entities
- Main locations
- Revenue range (if known)
- Growth plans (organic, acquisition, international)
- Current ERP * and version/age
- Other important systems (CRM, WMS, e-commerce, BI, payroll, etc.)
- Current implementation partner (if relevant)

## B. Sales opportunity
- Opportunity stage *: first introduction / discovery / demo / fit-gap / POC / final selection
- Expected decision date
- Known budget (range is fine)
- Estimated implementation size (S / M / L, users, entities)
- Competing solutions in the race
- Known decision criteria (and their weighting if known)
- Internal Deloitte opportunity owner

## C. Meeting setup
- Meeting date *
- Duration *
- In-person or remote *
- Demo language
- Number of attendees
- Client attendees (names/roles if known)
- Deloitte attendees and their roles in the session
- Decision makers present? (who)
- Technical stakeholders present? (who)

## D. Audience (repeat per important attendee) *
- Role and department
- Seniority
- Main objective ("what would make this person say yes")
- Main concern
- Likely objection
- Desired wow moment (what would visibly impress them)

## E. Business challenges *
- Current process (short narrative, per challenge)
- Main pain points
- Root causes (if known; otherwise mark "to diagnose")
- Business impact (time, money, risk, morale)
- Current workarounds (the Excel layer, the shadow systems)
- Desired future state
- Most urgent problem
- Most politically sensitive problem (handle with care in the room)

## F. Scope *
- In-scope business processes
- Explicitly out-of-scope processes
- Relevant Odoo applications (if the user already has a view; the brain validates it)
- Required integrations (systems that stay)
- Data migration expectations
- Localisation requirements (countries, statutory, payroll boundaries)
- Security requirements (roles, segregation, data sensitivity)
- Reporting requirements (operational, management, statutory)
- AI opportunities (what the client imagines; what they fear)

## G. Demo objectives *
- What must the client UNDERSTAND after the demo?
- What must the client BELIEVE after the demo?
- What must the client FEEL after the demo?
- What action should the client take next?
- Three required wow moments (from the client's perspective)
- Three topics that must NOT dominate the demo

## H. Constraints and risks
- Unclear requirements (list them; they become validation items)
- Unavailable data (what we cannot show realistically)
- Unstable demo functionality (known weak spots; plan backups)
- Unsupported assumptions currently circulating in the account team
- Client-specific customisation risks (expectations already set by others)
- Time constraints (preparation days available, environment availability)
- Sensitive commercial topics (pricing, licensing, competitor history)

---

## Completion behaviour
After any intake round, the brain must: (1) restate what it now knows in one compact block; (2) give the completeness score and band (file 0004); (3) list the highest-impact missing fields; (4) state what kind of output it can responsibly produce at this score. Never silently fill starred fields with guesses; industry defaults may be offered but must be labelled as defaults to confirm.
