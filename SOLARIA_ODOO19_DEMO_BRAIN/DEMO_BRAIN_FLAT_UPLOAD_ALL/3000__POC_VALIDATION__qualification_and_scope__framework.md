# POC Qualification and Scope Framework

Category: POC_VALIDATION. A POC is expensive proof; qualify before promising one.

## Demo versus POC (the distinction, stated plainly)
Demo: OUR data, OUR environment, rehearsed storyline; proves capability exists and fits the story.
POC: THEIR data, agreed success criteria, bounded scope; proves the open claims from the validation register.
Never call an extended demo a POC; never run a POC without signed criteria.

## Qualification gate (all five or no POC)
1. Real open claims exist that only client data can settle (validation register non-empty).
2. The client commits named people and hours, not just interest.
3. Success criteria can be written as measurable sentences the client will sign.
4. The outcome has commercial consequence (defined next step on success, defined stop on failure).
5. Scope fits a bounded effort (time-boxed; one to three claims, not a shadow implementation).

## Scope template (one page, signed)
- Claims under proof (from the register, verbatim, with labels)
- Success criteria (measurable, dated; see 3010)
- Client data: what, who provides, by when, handling rules (3040)
- Environment: edition (labelled), hosting, duration, teardown
- People: client roles and hours; Deloitte roles
- Explicit out-of-scope list (what this POC does NOT prove)
- Evaluation: date, audience, verdict format
- Consequence: next step on success; honest stop on failure

## Anti-patterns
Free-floating pilots without criteria; POCs sold to avoid a decision; scope creep absorbed silently (every added claim re-signs the scope); POC environments quietly becoming production.
