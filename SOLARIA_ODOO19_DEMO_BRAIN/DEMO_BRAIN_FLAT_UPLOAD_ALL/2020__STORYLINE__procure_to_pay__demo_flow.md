# Demo Storyline: Procure-to-Pay

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | STORYLINE (instantiates the 12-beat framework, foundation 0009) |
| Rules | Timing limits per format apply (0014); every beat needs data (0016) and rehearsal (0017); editions and claim labels always (0011, 0018) |

## Client problem
Maverick buying, chased approvals, bills that surprise finance.

## Audience
CFO, procurement lead, COO; 45 minutes

## Prerequisites
- purchase + stock + account rehearsed
- reordering rules staged
- approval story mapped honestly

## Modules (edition)
- purchase (C)
- stock (C)
- account (C)
- 3-way match, Approvals, OCR labelled (E)

## Start state
Stock below minimum on a hero SKU overnight

## End state
Vendor bill matched and scheduled for payment

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. The RFQ the system created overnight
2. vendor price comparison
3. confirm, the native approval step fires above threshold
4. receive the goods
5. the bill arrives, matches the PO (OCR beat labelled E if shown)
6. exception worklists: what is late, what is unbilled

## Wow moments
- The self-ordered RFQ
- approval state firing
- bill-to-PO match

## Challenger insight
Control is not more signatures; it is fewer surprises: matching and worklists control spend better than email chains.

## Likely questions
- Our approval matrix?
- vendor agreements?
- budget checks?

## Risks
Approval expectations exceeding Community honesty (say the ladder plainly)

## Validation points
- Matrix mapped line-by-line
- billing policy with finance
- replenishment parameters

## Short version (15 min)
Auto-RFQ to received to matched bill

## Executive version (30 min)
Frame with spend-control cost, run the arc, close on audit-trail value
