# Challenger Scenario: Implementation takes too long

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Timeline fear, often anchored on horror stories or a competitor's aggressive promise.

## Underlying concern
Business windows (season, contract dates); team bandwidth; credibility of plans.

## Challenger insight
Duration follows scope discipline more than vendor speed: phase-one minimalism (standard-first, migration restraint, custom gate) is what compresses timelines. Aggressive promises without scope discipline are how projects become the horror stories.

## Reframe
From 'how fast can you go' to 'what is the smallest valuable phase one, and what gates protect its date'.

## Recommended response pattern
- Refuse to counter-bid fantasy timelines
- present the phased approach with gates and what each phase delivers
- tie duration to their decisions (scope, data, availability) transparently

## Demo action
Show configuration speed live (the stage/report change) as evidence that standard-first moves fast

## Advancing question
Which business date really matters, and what is the minimum that must be live by then?

## Never say
Date promises in the demo room; 'faster than competitor X' claims

## Validation needed
A scoping exercise before any timeline; phase-one definition workshop
