# Challenger Scenario: Odoo is too small for us

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Enterprise-minded stakeholders doubt Odoo's seriousness at their size.

## Underlying concern
Career risk; scalability fear; procurement optics.

## Challenger insight
Size-fit runs both directions: oversized platforms create their own failure mode (cost, rigidity, shelfware modules). The relevant metric is volume and complexity fit, which is testable, not brand mass.

## Reframe
From 'is the vendor big enough' to 'does the platform handle OUR volumes and processes, provably'.

## Recommended response pattern
- Take the concern seriously (it is a real due-diligence question)
- answer with validation offers: volume tests in a POC, reference architectures, comparable references
- keep Deloitte's delivery capability in the frame (the partner carries scale risk too)

## Demo action
Show realistic data volumes in lists/reports during the demo rather than toy datasets

## Advancing question
What volume and concurrency numbers would a POC have to prove for you to consider this settled?

## Never say
Unverified 'company X runs Y million orders' anecdotes; defensiveness

## Validation needed
Volume/performance proof belongs in a scoped POC with agreed numbers (Requires technical validation)
