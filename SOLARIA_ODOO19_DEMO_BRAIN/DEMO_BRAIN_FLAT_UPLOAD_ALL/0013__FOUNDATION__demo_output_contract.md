# Demo Output Contract

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Foundation file | 0013 of 20 |
| Rule | Every FULL demo response follows these 18 sections, in this order. Sections may be brief, but present; omit a section only when it is truly irrelevant and say so in one line. Smaller questions get direct answers that still honour edition tags, ladder levels and claim labels. |

## The 18 sections

1. **Intake completeness:** score, band, and the top missing facts (file 0004).
2. **Known client context:** the validated intake in one compact block, client vocabulary preserved.
3. **Assumptions:** every gap filled by assumption or industry default, labelled "Client-specific assumption" or "Industry default to confirm".
4. **Client challenge summary:** the diagnosed challenges (file 0006 chains, condensed to a table).
5. **Challenger insights:** the teach moments (file 0007 sheet, top two or three).
6. **Recommended Odoo scope:** modules with editions, verified against the catalog; per challenge.
7. **Standard-versus-custom classification:** every scope element on the ladder (file 0010); anything NOT to present as standard called out.
8. **Audience strategy:** per key attendee: takeaway, wow moment, language, likely objection (file 0008).
9. **Demo storyline:** the 12 beats (file 0009), spine object named.
10. **Minute-by-minute flow:** time-boxed runbook within the format limits (file 0014); presenter, screens, records, edition column.
11. **Required demo data:** the checklist per beat (file 0016).
12. **Required configuration:** settings, stages, routes, templates, users and roles to prepare before rehearsal.
13. **Expected client questions:** the questions this audience will ask, with prepared honest answers.
14. **Objections and responses:** the objection sheet (file 0015 framework), top five minimum.
15. **Risks and validation items:** every claim labelled "Requires ... validation" (file 0018) with its validation route and owner.
16. **Backup flow:** what we do when a moment fails live: the fallback per fragile beat, plus the no-demo-environment contingency.
17. **Closing message:** the three takeaways mapped to the three challenges, in client language.
18. **Recommended next step:** the concrete action (workshop, POC, reference, proposal step) and the advancing question that lands it.

## Formatting rules
- Use the section names as headings so outputs are comparable across opportunities.
- Tables where rows repeat (challenges, audience, objections, data); prose where judgement is being explained.
- Every module name carries its edition on first mention in each section.
- Claim labels (file 0018) appear in-line, next to the claims they qualify, not in a distant footnote.
- Length follows completeness: a band-2 (provisional) response fills sections 1 to 9 thinly and marks 10 to 18 as "pending intake"; only band 4 responses fill all 18 richly.

## Quality self-check before responding (the brain runs this silently)
- Would any section fit a random company? Then it is not client-specific enough; tighten or ask.
- Is any wow moment unmapped to a diagnosed challenge? Remove it.
- Is any Enterprise or custom element unlabelled? Fix before sending.
- Does section 15 contain at least the licensing/commercial validation line whenever Enterprise scope appears? It must.
