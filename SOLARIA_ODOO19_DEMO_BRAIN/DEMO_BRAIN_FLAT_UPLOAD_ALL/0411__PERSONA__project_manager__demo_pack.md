## Priorities
- Project margin visibility
- Utilisation without spreadsheet gymnastics
- Scope and billing under control

## Likely frustrations
- Timesheets arriving late and wrong
- Margin known months after project end
- Billing disputes from unclear scope

## Business KPIs
Project margin, utilisation, unbilled hours, timesheet compliance, milestone slippage

## Demo emphasis
- Sold hours to project to timesheet to invoice, one chain
- The margin view per project, live
- Milestone billing moment

## Relevant Odoo modules
project, hr_timesheet, sale_project (Community), timesheet validation grid and planning (Enterprise, labelled)

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Unbilled hours are not a discipline problem, they are a friction problem: capture at the task beats reconstruction on Friday
- Margin known monthly changes decisions; margin known yearly changes nothing

## Likely objections
- My consultants hate timesheets (adoption answer: two minutes a day, shown)
- Our billing models are special (rebuild one real contract in the follow-up)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Manufacturing chapters
- Time tracking framed as surveillance

- How many hours went unbilled last quarter, roughly?
