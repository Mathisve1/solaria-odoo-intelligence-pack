# Persona Demo Pack: Operations Manager

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | PERSONA (extends foundation file 0008) |
| Rule | Refine with intake section D; this pack gives defaults, the intake gives truth. Foundation rules override. |

## Priorities
- Fewer fires today than yesterday
- Clear worklists for the team
- Less chasing people for status

## Likely frustrations
- Information living in colleagues' heads
- Systems that add clicks without removing work
- Being the human interface between departments

## Business KPIs
Backlog age, on-time completion, exception count per week, overtime hours

## Demo emphasis
- The day-in-the-life of their exact team: today's worklist, one exception handled, done
- Activity and escalation patterns (automation rules, Community)
- Before/after on one painful handoff

## Relevant Odoo modules
The operational module of their domain (stock, mrp, project, helpdesk or field service) plus chatter/activities as the follow-up engine

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Chasing status is a full-time job nobody was hired for; records that carry their own conversation remove it
- Worklists beat inboxes: the queue should find the person

## Likely objections
- Another system my team must learn (adoption; answer with the one-task-faster demo)
- Will it really match how we work? (configuration moment)

## Unsuitable demo content
- Strategy slides
- Executive dashboards
- Long configuration detours

## Opening questions
- What does your team chase most: information, approvals or materials?
- Show me the ugliest handoff in your week.

## Recommended closing
Name one task your team hates; next session we show exactly that task, timed, before and after.
