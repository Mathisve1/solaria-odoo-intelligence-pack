# Community and Enterprise: Demo Discipline

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Foundation file | 0011 of 20 |
| Basis | Source-verified fact from the Odoo 19.0 Intelligence Pack: Enterprise 19.0 is an add-ons-only layer on the Community code base |

## 1. The permanent facts
- **Community is the base layer:** the server, the framework and the core business apps (CRM, Sales, Invoicing, Inventory, Purchase, Manufacturing core, Project, core HR, Website, eCommerce, POS, automation rules).
- **Enterprise is the extension layer:** additional modules on top of Community. Enterprise never replaces Community modules; it extends them. Enterprise-only in 19.0 includes: Studio, full Accounting and statutory reporting, Payroll, Helpdesk, Field Service, Planning, Documents, Sign, Knowledge, Approvals, Subscriptions, Quality, Shop Floor, Barcode, advanced views (Gantt and others), and the entire native AI layer.
- The authoritative reference is the Community-versus-Enterprise map in the Intelligence Pack; the module catalog resolves any individual module's edition.

## 2. Edition rules for demo claims
1. **Every capability claim carries an edition**, internally always, and in the room whenever there is any risk of ambiguity. "Odoo does X" without an edition is a forbidden sentence in demo planning.
2. **Enterprise-dependent demo moments are labelled** in the storyline, the runbook and the room: a simple pattern is "this next part is Odoo Enterprise" said once, clearly, before the moment.
3. **Never demo Enterprise features into a Community-budget conversation** without the explicit framing that they are an upgrade path; expectation debt from unlabelled Enterprise demos is a classic deal-killer at contract stage.
4. **Demo environment edition is a planning decision** (workflow stage 11/12): decide deliberately whether the demo database is Community or Enterprise, matching the conversation; verify the edition during rehearsal (file 0017).

## 3. Licensing and entitlement caveats (always commercial, never asserted)
- The source proves which code ships in which edition; it does not prove what a specific client subscription includes. Pricing, user counts, app-based packaging and hosting entitlements are **commercial validation** items, always.
- Standard phrasing for the room: "This capability is part of Odoo Enterprise; we will confirm exactly what your subscription would include as part of the proposal."
- Hosting matters: some capabilities behave differently across Odoo Online, odoo.sh and on-premise (custom modules, AI prerequisites, integrations). Label hosting-dependent claims as validation items.

## 4. How to label Enterprise-dependent moments (practical patterns)
- In the storyline: suffix the beat with "(E)".
- In the runbook: an "edition" column per minute block.
- In the room: one sentence before the moment, no apology, value-first: "What you will see now is the Enterprise layer; it is exactly why it exists."
- In follow-up materials: an edition legend so nobody screenshots an Enterprise screen into a Community business case.

## 5. Forbidden patterns
- "It is all just Odoo" (edition laundering).
- Answering an edition question with licensing guesses.
- Mixing Community and Enterprise claims in one sentence without tags.
- Building the business case on features the proposed edition does not include.
The brain must never create edition ambiguity; when in doubt, it consults the catalog and says which edition, or flags the check.
