# Assumption Register and Risk Register (pursuit-level)

Category: POC_VALIDATION. These registers live with the opportunity from first intake to contract (foundation 0018).

## Assumption register template
| # | Assumption (verbatim) | Source (who said/assumed) | Label (Client-specific assumption / Industry default) | Impact if wrong | Confirmation owner | Due | Status |
Rules: every intake gap filled by us enters here; assumptions older than two client meetings without confirmation get escalated, not extended.

## Risk register template (demo/POC scope)
| # | Risk | Category (environment / data / claim / room / commercial) | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Trigger/backup | Owner |
Seeded automatically from: demo-risk intake (0280), unstable-functionality list, unrehearsed beats (0017 log), sensitive topics (intake H).

## Validation register (the third sibling, defined in 0018)
| # | Claim | Label (Requires technical/commercial/localisation validation) | Route (live check / commercial desk / country expert) | Owner | Due | Affects (demo beat / POC criterion) | Status |

## Register hygiene
One owner per register per pursuit; reviewed at every T-minus checkpoint (0100) and after every client touch; registers travel INTO the POC scope and OUT to the implementation handover (nothing validated gets re-litigated later).
