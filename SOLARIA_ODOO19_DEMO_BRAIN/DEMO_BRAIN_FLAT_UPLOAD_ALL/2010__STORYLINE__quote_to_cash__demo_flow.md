# Demo Storyline: Quote-to-Cash

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|

## Client problem
Quoting is slow and error-prone; the gap between sold and billed leaks margin.

## Audience
Sales director, CFO; 30 to 45 minutes

## Prerequisites
- sale + account rehearsed
- two real client pricing cases rebuilt
- invoicing policies configured both ways

## Modules (edition)
- sale (C)
- account (C)
- approvals or Studio rules for gates labelled (E) if shown

## Start state
Confirmed opportunity for the spine customer

## End state
Invoice paid; margin visible on the order

## Step-by-step demo flow
1. Quotation in two minutes with template
2. their pricing case computes correctly
3. discount touched, the control story told honestly
4. send, sign, pay online
5. ordered-vs-delivered invoicing explained on two products
6. invoice, payment, done

## Wow moments
- Their own pricing logic computing
- the policy toggle moment
- signature-to-confirmed

## Challenger insight
The first credible quote frames the deal: speed is a win-rate lever, not a convenience.

## Likely questions
- Approval gates?
- proforma and terms documents?
- multi-currency?

## Risks
Pricing case not rebuilt beforehand (never improvise pricing live)

## Validation points
- Three hardest pricing cases
- quote template legal review
- gate design (native step vs E options)

## Short version (15 min)
Template quote, price computes, portal signature

## Executive version (30 min)
Open with quote-turnaround cost, run the arc, close on cycle-time math
