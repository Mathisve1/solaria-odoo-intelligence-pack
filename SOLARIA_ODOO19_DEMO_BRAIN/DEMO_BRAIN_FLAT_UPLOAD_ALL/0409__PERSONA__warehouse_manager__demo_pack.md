## Priorities
- Pick accuracy and speed
- Painless counting
- Traceability without paperwork

## Likely frustrations
- Paper pick lists
- Wrong shipments and the blame that follows
- Counting weekends

## Business KPIs
Pick accuracy, lines per hour, inventory accuracy, dock-to-stock time, count effort

## Demo emphasis
- Receive-putaway-pick-ship on a real device (barcode app, Enterprise, labelled)
- The wrong-item scan objection beat
- A cycle count in sixty seconds

## Relevant Odoo modules
stock (Community: locations, routes, lots), stock_barcode (Enterprise), quality checks on receipt (Enterprise, labelled)

## Suitable Challenger insights
- Accuracy is a scanning-moment problem: catch errors at the shelf, not at the customer
- Counting pain is a process design smell; cycle counting kills the year-end weekend

## Likely objections
- Devices and wifi in our building? (honest validation answer, site survey)
- Speed versus our current muscle memory?

## Unsuitable demo content
- Abstract data models
- Finance chapters
- Mouse-driven warehouse demos

## Opening questions

- What happens when a new colleague picks alone for the first time?
