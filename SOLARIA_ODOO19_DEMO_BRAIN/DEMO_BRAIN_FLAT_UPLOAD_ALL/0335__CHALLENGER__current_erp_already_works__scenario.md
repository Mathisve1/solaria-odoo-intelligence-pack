# Challenger Scenario: Our current ERP already works

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
No burning platform; satisfaction with the status quo, or fatigue at the idea of change.

## Underlying concern
Change cost versus unclear gain; protecting teams from disruption.

## Challenger insight
Systems 'work' at a cost that stops being visible: the Excel shadow layer, the swivel-chair minutes, the reports rebuilt monthly. The status quo has a price tag nobody prints.

## Reframe
Quantify the invisible tax of 'working': hours in workarounds, decision latency, key-person risk. Then compare THAT to change cost.

## Recommended response pattern
- Respect the satisfaction (arguing with it loses)
- offer the workaround-audit: count the Excel files and re-typing loops around the ERP
- let their own numbers make the case or honestly close the door

## Demo action
Demo one workaround-heavy process before/after, timed

## Advancing question
If we counted the spreadsheets that orbit your ERP for one week, what do you guess we would find?

## Never say
'Your system is outdated' (they chose it); fear-mongering about end-of-life without facts

## Validation needed
The workaround audit itself; genuine willingness to conclude 'stay put' if numbers say so
