# Demo Openings and Closings

Extends foundation 0009 beats 1 to 5 and 11 to 12. Category: DEMO_METHOD.

## The opening (first 3 minutes decide attention)
1. One-line credential (who we are for THEM, not the firm history).
2. The promise: 'In the next 40 minutes you will see one of your orders travel from first contact to paid invoice, and where your three named pains disappear along the way.'
3. Context playback (from intake, their vocabulary): 45 seconds that buy trust.
4. The contract: agenda as business phases, Q&A policy ('interrupt for clarity, park depth'), and what we will NOT cover today (scope honesty up front).

## Opening anti-patterns
Firm history slides, apologising for the demo environment, asking 'what do you want to see?' live (that was intake's job), starting in a settings screen.

## The closing (the last 4 minutes decide momentum)
1. Replay: three takeaways mapped one-to-one to their three challenges, in their words.
2. Honesty summary: what still needs validation (say it proudly; it signals professionalism).
3. The advancing question (from the Challenger sheet), asked and then silence.
4. The concrete next step with a date attached, proposed rather than begged: 'We suggest a half-day workshop on your pricing and approvals within two weeks; who should be in the room?'

## Closing anti-patterns
'Any questions?' as the final sentence, closing without a next step, promising follow-ups without owners, letting the demo just... end.

## Overrun closing (90 seconds version)
Skip everything except: three takeaways, one validation honesty line, the next step with a date. Always deliverable, rehearse it as its own unit (0014 recovery rule).
