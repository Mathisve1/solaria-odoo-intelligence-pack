# Executive Demo Intake (30-minute format)

Use with the quick intake; adds the executive-specific angles. Category: CLIENT_INTAKE.

- Which executives exactly, and what does each measure their year by: ______
- The company's stated strategy (public or told): where must the demo connect to it: ______
- The one number leadership argues about most: ______
- Previous transformation history (successes and scars): ______
- Board/ownership context (PE-owned, family, listed) and its pressure direction: ______
- What would make the CEO lean forward (their words if known): ______
- What must NOT be said (pricing? headcount implications? a competitor's name?): ______
- Desired next step if the demo lands (workshop, POC, steering meeting): ______

Output feeds: storyline 2130 (executive overview) or the executive version of the chosen storyline; audience map for beats 1 to 5 emphasis.
