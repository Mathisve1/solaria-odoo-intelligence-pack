# Challenger Scenario: Migration is too risky

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Data and cutover fear, often after a painful previous migration.

## Underlying concern
Business interruption; data loss; the team's bandwidth.

## Challenger insight
Migration risk concentrates in scope ambition and rehearsal count, not in the act itself: migrating less history, twice-rehearsed, beats migrating everything once, hopefully.

## Reframe
From 'can we migrate safely' to 'what is the minimum data that must move, and how many rehearsals buy us certainty'.

## Recommended response pattern
- Present the mock-migration method (rehearsals with reconciliation gates)
- challenge history-migration maximalism (open items and balances move; archives can stay queryable)
- phase go-lives where topology allows

## Demo action
Show migrated-looking demo data quality: opening balances, open orders, master data in their vocabulary

## Advancing question
Which data, if it arrived clean on day one, would make go-live feel safe to your team?

## Never say
'Migration is easy'; timeline promises without an assessment

## Validation needed
Data-quality sampling and a migration assessment before any commitment (Requires technical validation)
