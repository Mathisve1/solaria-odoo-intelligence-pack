# Challenger Scenario: It is too expensive / cheaper alternatives exist

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER scenario (applies foundations 0007 and 0015) |
| Tone | Insight over argument; respectful always; competitor references high-level and validation-aware |

## Situation
Price pressure, sometimes anchored on Community being free or on a low-ball competitor.

## Underlying concern
Budget accountability; fear of hidden costs; comparing unlike scopes.

## Challenger insight
ERP cost lives in the implementation and the years after, not the licence line: integration tax, customisation debt and adoption failure dwarf subscription deltas. 'Free' Community has a real cost curve too, in the Enterprise capabilities it lacks (edition map honesty).

## Reframe
Compare total cost of outcome: licence plus implementation plus integration plus maintenance plus the cost of what the cheap option cannot do.

## Recommended response pattern
- Never defend price; restructure the comparison
- put the edition decision on the table honestly (what Community would and would not cover for them)
- commercial specifics to the commercial track (validation label)

## Demo action
Show one Enterprise-only capability they need with its edition label: make the delta concrete instead of abstract

## Advancing question
Shall we map your must-haves to editions so the price conversation compares real scopes?

## Never say
Discount improvisation; licensing numbers from memory; trash-talking cheap competitors

## Validation needed
All pricing through the commercial desk (Requires commercial validation, always)
