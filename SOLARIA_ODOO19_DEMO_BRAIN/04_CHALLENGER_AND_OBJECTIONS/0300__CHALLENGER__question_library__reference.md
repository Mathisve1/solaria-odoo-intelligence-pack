# Challenger Question Library

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER (applies foundation 0007) |
| Use | Pick 3 to 5 per session, tailored to intake; questions teach while they ask. |

## Cost-of-status-quo questions
- How many spreadsheets orbit your ERP right now, honestly?
- When did you last find out about a problem later than you should have, and what did the delay cost?
- Which numbers get rebuilt by hand every month, and by whom?
- What does a day of quote delay do to your win rate, in your own experience?

## Handoff and process questions
- Where does information get re-typed between systems in your order flow?
- Which handoff between departments loses the most context?
- What happens to an order when the one person who knows is on holiday?

## Decision-speed questions
- Which decision waited longest for data last quarter?
- If your management numbers were live instead of monthly, which meeting would change?

## Risk and control questions
- If an auditor asked for the full history of one transaction, how long would it take?
- Which approval today exists in email only?
- What would a recall or a compliance check look like with your current traceability?

## Future-state questions
- If one manual task disappeared this quarter, which would your team choose?
- What should a new employee be able to do alone in week one?

## AI-realism questions
- How many documents does your team type into systems per month?
- Who checks AI output today when someone quietly uses a chatbot for work?

## Usage rules
Ask, then be silent; the answer is the material. Never ask a question whose answer you will not use in the storyline. Log answers into intake sections E and G.
