# Challenger Insight Template

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | CHALLENGER (applies foundation 0007) |
| Use | One completed template per key challenge; feeds storyline beat 4 and the objection sheet. |

## The template (copy per insight)
```
Challenge (from diagnosis, file 0006): ...
Conventional client view: what they currently believe about it
Hidden cost / overlooked risk: what that belief costs, from THEIR numbers where possible
Useful business insight: the reframe, verifiable, specific to this client
Reframed problem: the problem actually worth solving
Relevant Odoo story: capability mapping with edition tags and claim labels (0018)
Live demonstration moment: which storyline beat makes it visible
Advancing question: the question that moves the sale after the moment lands
Tone check: would the decision maker feel smarter or caught out? (must be: smarter)
Evidence check: is the hidden cost derived or estimated? If estimated: label it and offer to validate together
```

## Quality bar (all four or rewrite)
1. Specific to this client (fails the any-company test).
2. Verifiable from their numbers or offered as a joint validation.
3. New to at least the decision maker.
4. Convertible: it ends in a demo moment and a question, not in applause.
