# Odoo Client Discovery Question Bank — Deloitte Playbook

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Document type | Strategy / Deloitte Advisory Playbook (discovery tool) |
| Authority level | High as a method tool; answers feed the fit-gap register |
| Usage | Pick relevant sections per engagement; every answer should map to a domain (04) and later a verdict (fit-gap methodology) |

## Executive discovery
1. Which three business outcomes must improve in 18 months (growth, margin, cycle time, compliance)?
2. Where does work cross the most systems/spreadsheets today? What does that cost?
3. Which decisions are slow because data is late or distrusted?
4. Appetite for process change vs software adaptation — who arbitrates when they conflict?
5. Countries/entities in scope now and in 3 years (M&A pipeline)?
6. What's the hosting/sovereignty stance (cloud, on-prem, region)?
7. Who owns master data today — and who will?
8. What defines "go-live success" in numbers?

## Sales process
1. Channels (direct, web, POS, marketplaces, EDI) and their volumes?
2. Pricing logic: lists, customer-specific, promotions, rebates — worst real example?
3. Quotation flow: who approves what (amounts, discounts)? Blocking or advisory?
4. Configurable products? How deep (variants vs constraint logic)?
5. Invoicing policy: on order or delivery? Down payments? Consolidated invoices?
6. Portal expectations (customers see/do what)?

## Finance / Accounting
1. Countries, GAAPs, statutory reports, e-invoicing mandates per country?
2. Close process today: days, pain points, who does what?
3. Banking: banks, formats, reconciliation volumes, payment runs (SEPA/batch)?
4. AR: dunning practice, credit control? AP: approval and 3-way-match needs?
5. Assets, budgets, consolidation, deferrals — which exist, where?
6. Multi-currency/multi-company flows incl. inter-company volume?
7. Audit/inalterability requirements per jurisdiction?
8. Open-item and opening-balance migration expectations?

## Inventory / Warehouse
1. Sites, warehouses, internal flows; 3PL involvement?
2. Receipt-to-ship steps today (1/2/3-step, QC gates)?
3. Traceability needs (lots/serials/expiry) and recall drills?
4. Replenishment logic (min/max, forecast, MTO) — who decides today?
5. Barcode/hardware landscape; carrier contracts?
6. Valuation method and finance alignment?
7. Inventory accuracy today (measured)?

## Purchase
1. Approval matrix (amounts × categories × entities × delegation)?
2. Vendor pricing agreements, blanket orders, tenders?
3. Receipt/bill matching practice; touchless-AP ambition?
4. Direct vs indirect spend split; who buys what?

## Manufacturing
1. Discrete/process/mixed? MTS/MTO/ETO mix?
2. BoM depth, variants, engineering-change practice (who, how often)?
3. Scheduling expectation: sequencing, capacity, promise dates — precisely which?
4. Shop-floor data capture today (paper, MES, terminals)?
5. Quality regime (gates, certificates, regulated?)
6. Subcontracting flows? Costing model (standard/actual, overheads)?

## HR / Recruitment
1. Countries × headcount; payroll today (in-house/bureau) and its boundary tomorrow?
2. Time off/attendance rules complexity (unions, shifts)?
3. Expense volumes and policy pain?
4. Hiring volume, channels, assessment steps; works-council constraints?
5. Employee/candidate data privacy constraints and retention rules?
6. Self-service ambitions (what may employees do)?

## Project / Services
1. Billing models (fixed, T&M, milestone, retainer) with contract examples?
2. Utilization/planning practice; who schedules?
3. Timesheet culture today (compliance %)?
4. Project profitability: is it measured, and against what cost rates?
5. Customer collaboration expectations (portal, reports)?

## Field Service
1. Intervention types, volumes, geography; technician count?
2. Scheduling/dispatch practice; optimization ambition (assignment vs routing)?
3. Worksheets/proof-of-service requirements (legal?)
4. Parts logistics (van stock, returns)?
5. Device/connectivity reality in the field?

## Website / eCommerce
1. Sites/brands/languages; who edits content today?
2. B2B, B2C or both? Customer-specific pricing online?
3. Payment providers and fees; checkout must-haves (legal fields, delivery options)?
4. Catalog size/content readiness; PIM in place?
5. SEO baseline to protect; traffic volumes?

## Reporting / BI
1. Which 10 reports run the business (get samples!)?
2. Who builds reports today; self-service ambition?
3. Corporate BI landscape (warehouse, tools) — boundary for Odoo data?
4. Real-time vs monthly needs per audience?

## Security / Access
1. Roles today; SoD requirements (audit findings?)
2. Multi-company visibility rules?
3. Admin governance and change control?
4. External access (portal, auditors, partners)?

## AI opportunities (pair with AI strategy playbook)
1. Which repetitive text/document tasks eat hours (bills, CVs, replies, filing)?
2. Where do exceptions hide until too late (margin, SLA, credit, stock)?
3. What would a role assistant need to know to save each persona 30 min/day?
4. Data governance posture: what may leave the environment? Who signs off AI use?
5. Regulated AI areas in scope (recruitment!) and their compliance owner?

## Customization-risk probes (ask early, save budgets)
1. "Which parts of your current system did you customize — and would you again?"
2. For each requested customization: what business outcome, how often, who owns it at every upgrade?
3. Which legacy behaviors are actually contractual/legal vs habitual?
4. Who has authority to accept a process change instead of a build?

**Usage note:** log every answer against a domain and a requirement ID; unanswered = discovery debt, not assumptions.
