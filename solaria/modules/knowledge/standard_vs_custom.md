# Knowledge (`knowledge`) — Standard vs Configuration vs Studio vs Custom — Odoo 19.0

Edition: Enterprise-only.

## Likely standard (Enterprise)
Hierarchical articles with rich editor · workspace/private/shared with member permissions · templates & categories · article threads/comments · embedded live business views · favorites, trash/restore · portal/website publishing · helpdesk insertion · AI text drafts.

## Configuration possibilities
Taxonomy, templates, permission defaults, stages for editorial flow, public help-center scope.

## Studio possibilities
Essentially none needed — resist adding fields to articles; structure lives in taxonomy.

## Automation possibilities
Stale-article review activities, onboarding reading assignments, digest of new/changed articles.

## Custom development is justified when
- Almost never for v1. Usage analytics or compliance-grade controlled docs → consider documents/sign or external tools first.

## External integration is justified when
- Corporate intranet remains for comms; LMS for formal training — set boundaries, don't duplicate content.

## What to avoid
- Importing the whole legacy wiki (dead content kills trust).
- No named owners (the #1 failure mode).
- Grounding AI on ungoverned articles — garbage in, confident garbage out.

## Deloitte recommendation principles
Treat Knowledge as governance+adoption consulting with a light tool layer; tie it to the AI strategy (curated corpus) to make content quality a business case.

## Validation questions
1. Which knowledge domains, which owners, which review cadence?
2. What must be public (help center) vs internal?
3. Is AI grounding intended — then who certifies content?
