## The one-page summary

We converted the **actual source code of Odoo 19.0** — all 1,422 Community and Enterprise modules — into a curated, evidence-graded knowledge base and configured Solaria with it. Solaria now reasons like a **senior Odoo functional analyst, solution architect, fit-gap analyst, demo coach and implementation advisor**: business-first, standard-before-custom, always distinguishing Community from Enterprise, and always marking what is source-verified versus what needs live validation. It is not a chatbot with opinions about Odoo; it is an advisor grounded in what verifiably ships in 19.0.

## Why this matters for Deloitte

1. **Speed with credibility.** Fit-gap orientation, edition decisions, demo storylines and discovery preparation that previously took senior-consultant days are available in minutes — with evidence discipline built in (it cites which document supports each claim).
2. **Consistency.** Every consultant gets the same standard-first, edition-explicit advisory stance — the stance that protects clients from customization debt and protects Deloitte from overpromise disputes.
3. **Partner positioning.** Deloitte's Odoo story is strategy + compliance + transformation + implementation. A source-grounded internal advisor is itself proof of engineering seriousness — and it encodes our positioning playbook.
4. **AI leadership, honestly.** Odoo 19 Enterprise ships a real native AI layer (verified at module level: agents, AI fields, document digitization and more — Enterprise-only). Solaria knows exactly what ships versus what is a Deloitte-designed concept (Company Brain, Operations Copilot), so our AI conversations stay credible.

## What Solaria can now do (ask it these things)
- Map a client problem to Odoo modules with edition and licensing-validation flags.
- Classify requirements: standard / configuration / Studio / automation / custom / integration — with reasons and rejected alternatives.
- Draft demo storylines per audience, discovery question sets per domain, and roadmap skeletons with decision gates.
- Answer "what does Enterprise actually add in domain X?" from source evidence rather than folklore.
- Tell you what it does **not** know and what must be validated in a live Odoo environment — by design.

## How to use it in client conversations (and how not to)
- **Do:** prepare with it (pursuit prep, workshop design, fit-gap orientation, objection handling), then let humans validate the load-bearing claims in a demo database before commitments.
- **Do not:** paste its output into proposals unreviewed, quote licensing/pricing from it (out of scope by design), or treat its behavior-level statements as tested fact — source analysis proves *existence*, live validation proves *behavior*.

## Key risks and guardrails (already engineered in)
- **Overpromise risk** → controlled evidence vocabulary; every behavior claim carries a validation caveat; a "generic answer kill-switch" forbids empty flexibility talk.
- **Edition confusion** → Community/Enterprise tagging is mandatory in its answers; subscription contents always flagged for commercial validation.
- **AI hype risk** → a dedicated governance document defines demo-safe and client-safe phrasing; concepts are never presented as product.
- **Staleness** → scope is pinned to Odoo 19.0 with a dated Enterprise snapshot; refresh is a defined maintenance activity.

## How to avoid overpromising (the three sentences to internalize)
1. "Source-verified" means *it exists in 19.0* — not that it behaves exactly as hoped at the client.
2. Anything touching licensing, statutory compliance or go-live commitments needs expert/commercial validation — Solaria will say so; keep that sentence in the deliverable.
3. Deloitte AI concepts are our designs on Odoo's platform — never "an Odoo feature".
