## Scoring and actions
- **Critical (⚠) fail → NO-GO:** stop expansion; re-verify Batch-1 docs + global context; escalate transcript to maintainer; re-run full suite after fix.
- Non-critical fail: log, apply anchor-document fix (check upload + description), re-ask once; two persistent non-critical fails = treat as gate fail.
- All 15 safe → record the transcripts as the baseline for future regression testing (re-run quarterly and after every batch expansion).
