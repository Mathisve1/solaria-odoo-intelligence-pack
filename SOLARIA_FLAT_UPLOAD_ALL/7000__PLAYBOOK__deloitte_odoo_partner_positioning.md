# Deloitte Odoo Partner Positioning — Playbook

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Document type | Strategy / Deloitte Advisory Playbook (internal framing) |
| Authority level | High for positioning narrative; not proof of product features |
| Handling | Internal Deloitte framing — adapt tone before client-facing use |

## 1. Why Odoo matters strategically
- **Mid-market ERP consolidation:** one suite replacing 5–10 point tools (CRM, ERP, e-commerce, POS, HR, DMS, e-sign) at mid-market economics — a segment historically underserved between SME accounting tools and tier-1 ERP.
- **Open-source core + commercial Enterprise layer** (verified in this pack: Enterprise is an add-on layer on the Community code base): transparency, no black box, huge ecosystem, fast release cadence.
- **Platform data-coherence:** one data model across front- and back-office — the prerequisite for the AI-in-ERP wave (and Odoo 19 Enterprise already ships a native AI layer).
- For Deloitte: an offering for clients priced out of S/4-scale programs, and a land-and-expand vehicle (start with one domain, grow across the suite).

## 2. The Deloitte difference (more than an implementation partner)
Position Deloitte as **strategy + transformation + implementation + assurance** on Odoo:
1. **Business-first advisory** — process redesign and operating-model work before configuration (this knowledge pack is built that way: standard-first, evidence-based).
2. **Industry depth** — Deloitte industry blueprints turn a horizontal suite into vertical answers.
3. **Compliance muscle** — tax/audit/legal network validates localizations, statutory reporting, e-invoicing mandates, AI governance (EU AI Act) — the areas where pure-play integrators are thinnest.
4. **Transformation discipline** — change management, adoption, governance, PMO for multi-country rollouts.
5. **AI strategy credibility** — govern and design AI-in-ERP (native layer + Deloitte concepts like Company Brain/Copilots) rather than demo-ware.

## 3. Speaking to executives (CEO/CFO/COO)
- Lead with consolidation economics (license+integration+maintenance of the current stack vs one platform) and cycle-time stories.
- Edition framing: "Community proves the platform; Enterprise operationalizes finance close, compliance, and AI" — honest, evidence-backed (see 03 map).
- Risk posture: standard-first implementation = lower TCO and painless upgrades; Deloitte enforces it contractually (fit-gap discipline).
- AI: pragmatic — native assists now (OCR, drafting, agents), governed copilots next; no moonshots before master-data hygiene.

## 4. Speaking to functional teams
- "Your process, shown live" — demos in their vocabulary (demo playbook), configuration reveals ("that's a setting").
- Respect their expertise: fit-gap workshops where their exceptions are heard, then challenged constructively.
- Promise: fewer swivel-chair tasks, one truth, exceptions surfaced automatically.

## 5. Speaking to technical teams
- Architecture honesty: Python/PostgreSQL, ORM, inheritance-based extension, real API surface; Enterprise = add-ons, not a fork (source-verified).
- Their new role: guardians of standard, owners of the (small) custom inventory, integration architects — not ticket-closers for a black box.
- Governance artifacts Deloitte brings: customization registry, upgrade playbook, environment strategy, Studio governance.

## 6. Standard-first as a positioning asset
Say it explicitly in pursuits: "We will challenge your customization requests." Clients have been burned by yes-integrators; the discipline is a differentiator. Back it with the framework (05) and fit-gap methodology artifacts.

## 7. Competitive framing (fair, not disparaging)
- vs tier-1 ERP: speed, cost, usability; concede extreme-scale/industry-depth cases honestly.
- vs point-tool stacks: integration tax, data silos, AI-readiness of one coherent platform.
- vs pure-play Odoo integrators: compliance network, industry blueprints, transformation scale, AI governance.
- Never claim feature superiority without evidence from this pack or a live validation.

## 8. Engagement patterns
1. **Odoo Fit Assessment** (2–4 weeks): discovery + fit-gap on priority domains + edition/licensing recommendation + roadmap — productized entry.
2. **Pilot/prototype sprint:** one domain live-configured on client data; decision evidence.
3. **Phased implementation** (roadmap playbook), **multi-country rollout**, **AI-in-ERP strategy overlay**, **post-go-live value & governance retainers**.

## 9. Proof points to build (practice development)
Reference demos per industry, localization validation notes per priority country, AI governance starter kit, upgrade-experience metrics from delivered projects — feed them back into this knowledge pack each iteration.

## 10. Cautions
- Licensing/pricing statements always via current Odoo commercial terms — never from this pack.
- Partner-tier claims, marketplace numbers, Odoo roadmap promises: validate before use.
- Client-facing derivatives of this document must drop internal competitive framing.
